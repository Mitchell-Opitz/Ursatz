# Shared primitives

The small public helpers that every layer above reuses instead of writing its own copy. [Docs index](README.md)

Small public helpers that every layer above reuses instead of writing its own copy.

- `core/string_copy.h`: `ursatz_string_copy`.
- `core/rational.h`: `Rational`, a signed exact rational on 64-bit integers, always in lowest terms. Every operation is checked and reports `RATIONAL_ERR_OVERFLOW`; nothing wraps. `MusicalTime` and `Duration` each hold one as `.value`.
- `time/meter_arithmetic.h`: exact measure and beat functions over a `MeterMap` (measure of a time, measure bounds, beat length, beat of a time, snap to beat, on beat), on `Rational`. The standard-form, standard-meter and standard-texture time helpers wrap it. `time_span_holds` in `time/time_span.h` is the shared span check.
- `pitch/pitch_arithmetic.h`: letter semitone and index, letter offset, pitch class, line-of-fifths position, spelling names and parsing. Theory-neutral; every framework's letter tables sit on it. The one name table is the line of fifths from -6 to 10: `pitch_class_spelling` and `pitch_class_window_name` read a pitch class inside a window of 12 fifths (sharps at -1, flats at -6, a major tonic at -5, a minor tonic at -3), `pitch_class_name` and `key_tonic_name` follow. `pitch_name_of_midi` writes a name with its octave (`midi / 12 - 1`, C's truncating division), `pitch_class_step` the shortest signed step between two pitch classes (the tritone up or down as the caller says), and `pitch_class_of_midi` is the pitch class of any MIDI number (never `((x % 12) + 12) % 12`). Key, mode and degree functions are `theory/tonality/tonal_key.h`.
- `io/midi_importer.h`: `midi_importer_import_complete` parses an SMF once and returns notes, meter, tempo, key signatures, and every track's name and program changes in one `MidiImport`. `smf_reader.h` and `midi_event_decoder.h` are internal to `io`.
- `theory/tonality/roman_numeral_grammar.h`: the Roman numeral grammar. `standard_tonality_numeral_parse` reads every numeral form (alteration, case, quality mark, figure, applied target), `standard_tonality_numeral_format` writes the canonical spelling, `standard_tonality_numeral_root_semitone` gives the root through an applied target. The older numeral entry points are wrappers on it. standard-harmony keeps its own label writer, checked by a test.
- `semantics/analysis_note.h`, `analysis_segment.h`, `voice_grid.h`: the neutral analysis input types (`AnalysisNote`, `AnalysisSegment`, `SpelledKey`, `AnalysisVoiceGrid`) that framework adapters accept, so a caller no longer writes the converter. See `docs/modules/semantics.md`.
- `theory/tonality/triad_rules.h`, `seventh_rules.h`: `standard_tonality_triad_quality_semitones` and `standard_tonality_seventh_quality_semitones`, the one table of the intervals of each chord quality above its root (R-686). The chord vocabulary, the harmony segment shapes, the applied chords, the numeral adapter and the motive sketch take their intervals from them; the test `standard_tonality_quality_semitones` pins them to the `chord-type` entries of the knowledge snapshot.
- `theory/tonality/chord_vocabulary.h`: `common_practice_chord_symbol_suffix`, the symbol suffix of a quality name ("m", "7", "maj7", "m7", "dim", "dim7", "m7b5", "aug"). The Analyzer's text export prints chord symbols with it.
- `theory/tonality/roman_numeral_rules.h`: `standard_tonality_roman_numeral_letters`, the length of the leading run of numeral letters (i, v, I, V), for a scan that keeps its own rule for what surrounds the run. `theory/tonality/interval.h`: `standard_tonality_interval_of_size` and `standard_tonality_generic_size_of_semitones` read an interval from a size or from semitones alone. `theory/tonality/tonal_key.h`: `standard_tonality_mask_step`, the next pitch up or down whose pitch class is in a mask; each caller keeps its own mask.
- `tools/ci/check-primitives.sh` fails on a copy of any of these outside its owner: pitch letters C C D, an octave from `pitch_class_name` and `/ 12 - 1`, a chord-quality interval set, a scan of the numeral letters, a pitch class by `((x % 12) + 12) % 12` and the chord symbol suffixes (R-688).
