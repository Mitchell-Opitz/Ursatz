# time

Layer L2 (`music-time`). Path `src/time/`, tests `tests/time/`. Depends on `identity` and `core` only. Sibling of `pitch`; the two do not depend on each other.

## Provides

`Duration` (magnitude), `MusicalTime` (position), `TimeSpan` (a positioned interval between two `MusicalTime`s), `TimeInstant` (a zero-duration point wrapping `MusicalTime`), and `TemporalAnchor` (wraps a `MusicalTime`, situates a higher-layer object at that position). All rational quantities are exact, never float or double (Law 19).

`MetronomeMarking` (a beat unit plus beats per minute, both exact rationals), `HistoricalPeriod` (a named stretch of calendar years, optionally open-ended), `NoteValue` (a written rhythmic value, breve through 1024th, with up to four dots), `TimeSignature` (a top number and an undotted bottom unit), and `Tuplet` (a ratio of notes played to notes replaced), `MeterMap` (the time signatures of a piece laid over its time), `PerformanceTime` (an exact rational count of seconds), `TempoMap` (the mapping from `MusicalTime` to seconds), and `MetricalPosition` (where a time falls in a measure) are also provided.

## Notes

- `Duration` and `MusicalTime` are structurally identical (numerator/denominator, reduced to lowest terms) but are separate, non-convertible types. One is a magnitude, the other a position. There is no conversion between them, and none should be added.
- `TimeSpan`'s `boundary` vocabulary (`closed` / `open` / `left_closed` / `right_closed`) is provisional per Domain Spec §16.3, not frozen. It is implemented as given; a later revision to that vocabulary is not a breaking-change violation.
- `TimeSpan` requires `boundary` to be supplied explicitly at construction. There is no default. A deserializer built on top of `time_span_create` must reject a payload that omits it rather than assuming `closed`.
- `MusicalTime` has no lower bound enforced at this layer. Negative positions (pickup measures, for example) are legal here. What a negative position means is deferred to a later spec.
- `time/meter_arithmetic.h` is the exact measure and beat arithmetic over a `MeterMap`, on `Rational` (whole notes, no floating point). `meter_map_measure_of`, `meter_map_last_measure_touched` and `meter_map_measure_bounds` locate a time (a barline belongs to the measure that starts there). `meter_map_beat_length` is the time signature's unit, or three units in a compound meter (a top number of 6, 9 or 12 over an eighth or smaller unit); `meter_map_beat_count` counts the beats that start in a measure (a short measure and a pickup count their real length, rounded up). `meter_map_beat_of` gives measure, 1-based beat and the offset from that beat; `meter_map_snap_to_beat` gives the first beat at or after a time, never past the measure end; `meter_map_on_beat` and `meter_map_first_counted_measure` complete the set. Every function returns a `MeterMapResult`, writes its outputs only on success, and reports `METER_MAP_ERR_OVERFLOW` (new, additive) where an exact value does not fit; a negative time is `METER_MAP_ERR_TIME_BEFORE_START`. A framework that needs a measure or beat computation wraps these and does not write its own.
- `time/score_time.h` is the one kernel owner of the measure, beat and snap reads an analysis adapter needs (R-288): `score_time_span` (a `TimeSpan` as two `Rational`s), `score_time_measure_bounds`, `score_time_beat_length` (both over `meter_arithmetic.h`) and `score_time_snap` (the nearest multiple of 1/grid within 1/tolerance, a half rounding up). Each returns `bool`, false when an argument is invalid or a value does not fit a `Rational`, and writes its outputs only on true. There is no unchecked fallback. The standard-meter, standard-form and standard-texture time modules wrap these and keep their signatures until they are retired; their plain-value operators return 0/1 on overflow. `MeterMap` itself computes on `Rational` and reports `METER_MAP_ERR_OVERFLOW` from creation and lookups where a grid step does not fit.
- `time_span_is_valid` (`time/time_span.h`): true when both ends exist and the end is strictly after the start, compared exactly. It replaces the per-framework span checks.
- `TemporalAnchor` carries no fields beyond the wrapped `MusicalTime`. It exists so callers building on `Occurrence` (L1) gain a temporal position without embedding position semantics into `Occurrence` itself.
- `MetronomeMarking` derives seconds from the marking (one beat unit lasts 60/bpm seconds) as an exact rational, never float/double (Law 19). Textual tempo words are not metronome markings; they live in `events/tempo_marking.h` and `frameworks/standard-markings/`.
- `HistoricalPeriod` is historical time, not musical time, and has nothing to do with `MusicalTime`/`Duration`. Years are inclusive on both ends, the end year is optional so a period still going never needs updating, and periods may overlap -- nothing here resolves that. The conventional periods themselves are `frameworks/western-periods/`.
- `NoteValue` is notation vocabulary, not a magnitude: it holds a power-of-two exponent (breve -1, whole 0, half 1, quarter 2, ...) and a dot count, and converts one way to a `Duration` (`note_value_duration`, exact, dots included). There is no conversion back, because many written values share a length. It carries no names, flags, or glyph conventions; those are `frameworks/standard-rhythm/`.
- `TimeSignature` is exactly the two numbers of a time signature: a top number (1 to 64) and an undotted bottom unit from the whole note to the sixteenth. It claims nothing about beats. In a simple meter the top counts beats over a beat unit; in a compound meter it counts divisions (6/8 is two beats of three eighths), and that reading is `frameworks/standard-meter/`. It is not a fraction: 2/4 and 4/8 have the same measure length (`time_signature_measure_duration`, top times the unit's length, true for simple and compound alike) but are not equal. A dotted bottom unit is rejected. `Measure` names a `MeterMap` region by `EntityID` and resolves it against the map (`containers/measure.h`); see `MeterMap` below.
- `Tuplet` is only a ratio: `actual` notes in the time of `normal` (3:2, 2:3, 5:4). It holds no note value or position; `tuplet_note_duration` and `tuplet_span` scale a `Duration` exactly. The counts are bounded (2-64 and 1-64) and unequal, and the ratio is not reduced, so 6:4 and 3:2 differ. Names and the written number are `frameworks/standard-rhythm/`; which meter a tuplet is borrowed from is `frameworks/standard-meter/`.
- `MeterMap` (`time/meter_map.h`) is the single source for measure boundaries: an ordered, non-overlapping, gap-free list of regions, each `{TimeSignature, start, end or open, first measure number}`. It holds meter only, makes no claim about beats or beat strength, and does not read a signature as simple or compound (that is `frameworks/standard-meter/`). It depends on `identity` and `time` only.
  - **Regions and measures.** The first region starts at 0 and the last is open. A region's measures are laid from its start in steps of its signature's measure duration. If the next region starts before the last measure is complete, that measure is a short measure: it counts as one measure, ends where the next region begins, and the next region starts a fresh grid there. A change point repeating the previous signature on one of its barlines adds nothing and is dropped; mid-measure it is kept and ends a short measure.
  - **Numbering.** Measure numbering follows the score: the first full measure is 1, numbers run on across regions, and a short measure keeps its number (a 4/4 measure cut short by a 3/4 change is still measure N, and the 3/4 region begins at N+1).
  - **Pickup.** A pickup is measure 0 and is detected only when the file says so, never from note content. The first region is a pickup if a region follows it and either its length is less than one measure of its own signature (a partial first measure ended by a later time signature event), or it is exactly one measure of its own signature, differs from the next signature, and its measure is less than half of the next region's measure (a short leading signature such as 1/8 or 3/8 before 12/8). The half threshold is a convention: it keeps a genuine 3/4 first bar before a 4/4 from reading as a pickup.
  - **Barlines.** A barline time belongs to the following measure: spans are left-closed, `[start, end)`, and `meter_map_measure_index_at(barline)` is the measure that starts there. `meter_map_measure_count(end_time)` counts the measures that start before `end_time` (a pickup included), so an end on a barline adds no empty measure and an end inside a measure counts that measure.
  - **Defaulted.** A region is defaulted when the file declared no signature for it. `meter_map_create_defaulted` makes one 4/4 region marked defaulted; if the first declared signature is after tick 0, the span before it is a defaulted 4/4 region. `meter_map_is_defaulted` is true when every region is, so a consumer can tell a declared meter from a default.
  - **Lookups.** `meter_map_region_at`, `meter_map_measure_index_at`, `meter_map_measure_span` (a new left-closed `TimeSpan`; a short measure's span is its real extent), `meter_map_region_of_measure`, `meter_map_measure_count`, and `meter_map_region_by_id`. Each region has its own `EntityID`, which is what `Measure`'s `meter_map_region_ref` holds. Change points must be strictly ascending: two at one time are an error, since there is no way to say which wins.
- `PerformanceTime` (`time/performance_time.h`) is a count of seconds as an exact rational in lowest terms: a pure value with no theory, never floating point, never negative. It is kept apart from `Duration` on purpose: a `Duration` is symbolic (whole notes) and does not depend on tempo; a `PerformanceTime` is what a tempo makes of it. It has value equality, an exact `performance_time_compare` that cannot overflow, and a checked `performance_time_add`: a result that does not fit is `PERFORMANCE_TIME_ERR_OVERFLOW`, never a wrapped number.
- `TempoMap` (`time/tempo_map.h`) maps `MusicalTime` to performance time (seconds): an ordered, gap-free list of regions, each `{start, microseconds per quarter note}`, the first from 0 and the last open. It is the L2 mapping for a later MIDI renderer to read through the region list; it renders and exports nothing. It depends on `identity` and `time` only.
  - **Symbolic time is tempo-independent.** A `MusicalTime` or `Duration` means the same with or without a `TempoMap`. The map only answers how many seconds that is. It is also not a tempo marking: `MetronomeMarking` and `TempoMarking` are notation-level values and are neither replaced nor produced here.
  - **Exact seconds.** For a whole-note span `d` at `T` microseconds per quarter note, `seconds = d * 4 * T / 1,000,000`. Seconds at a time are that summed over every region before it plus the part of its own region reached (`tempo_map_seconds_at`); `tempo_map_duration_seconds(span)` is seconds at the end less seconds at the start, across any number of changes, and does not read the span's boundary type. All of it is exact rational arithmetic on unsigned 64-bit integers in lowest terms, never floating point.
  - **Overflow.** Every product and sum is checked in plain C: a value that does not fit returns `TEMPO_MAP_ERR_OVERFLOW`, never a wrapped number. A map whose seconds at some change point would not fit is rejected when built.
  - **Inverse.** `tempo_map_time_at_seconds` is the exact inverse: within the region that holds the seconds, `time = start + (seconds - seconds at start) * 1,000,000 / (4 * T)`. So `time_at_seconds(seconds_at(t))` is `t` exactly, for every `t`.
  - **Regions and default tempo.** A change point is a region and none is merged: a tempo repeating the previous one is still its own region, so the list says what the file said. Change points must be strictly ascending (two at one time leave no way to say which wins), and a tempo of 0 is an error. A change point belongs to the region it starts (`tempo_map_tempo_at` at a change point is the new tempo). If no tempo is declared at 0, the MIDI default of 500,000 microseconds per quarter note (120 quarter notes per minute) covers the span before the first one as a defaulted region; `tempo_map_create_defaulted` is one defaulted region from 0. `tempo_map_is_defaulted` is true when every region is, so a consumer can tell a declared tempo from a default. Each region has its own `EntityID` (`tempo_map_region_by_id`), as `MeterMap`'s do.
- `MetricalPosition` (`time/metrical_position.h`) is where a `MusicalTime` falls in a measure: `{measure, offset}`, the measure number as `MeterMap` numbers it (the first full measure is 1, a pickup is 0) and an offset into that measure, an exact `Duration` in whole notes from the measure's start. It is pure arithmetic and makes no beat claim: it holds no `TimeSignature`. The roadmap's "measure, beat, subdivision" is split across layers: the kernel value is measure + offset, and beat and subdivision are `frameworks/standard-meter`'s reading of that offset (`standard_meter_beat_position`), with a `BeatStrength` from `standard_meter_beat_strength`. The kernel never includes standard-meter.
  - **Derivation.** `metrical_position_from_time(map, time, &position)` and its inverse `metrical_position_to_time` use a `MeterMap`; `to_time(from_time(t))` is `t`, and `from_time(to_time(p))` is `p`. All arithmetic is exact and checked: a value that does not fit, including a measure number past `int`, is `METRICAL_POSITION_ERR_OVERFLOW`, never a wrapped number. A time before 0 is an error. A `MeterMap` is open-ended, so there is no "past the end" error.
  - **Offset semantics.** The offset is never negative and is less than the length of the measure it is in. In a short measure (cut short by a mid-measure meter change) the offset runs from where that measure started and only up to its real, short length; `to_time` rejects an offset past it.
  - **Barlines.** A time exactly on a barline is the next measure at offset 0 (a barline belongs to the following measure, as in `MeterMap`): one whole note in 4/4 is measure 2, offset 0.
  - **Pickups.** A pickup is measure 0, and its offset is measured from where a full measure would have started, as in a score: a one-quarter pickup in 4/4 is at offset 3/4 (beat 4), and an eighth-note pickup in 6/8 at 5/8. The full measure is the one the file implies. A partial first measure ended by a later time signature uses its own signature. A short leading signature (a 1/8 region before 12/8) uses the following region's: the pickup eighth is at offset 11/8 of a 12/8 measure. `metrical_position_reading_signature(map, measure, &signature)` returns the signature a measure's positions are read in, which is the one to pass to standard-meter. No pickup is inferred from note content.
- **Exact arithmetic and overflow.** Arithmetic on time values is `core/rational.h` (Law 19). `time/time_rational.h` reads a `MusicalTime`, `Duration` or `PerformanceTime` as a `Rational` (`RATIONAL_ERR_OVERFLOW` when a denominator exceeds `LLONG_MAX`) and builds new owned values from one (`TIME_RATIONAL_ERR_OVERFLOW` when the fields do not fit, `TIME_RATIONAL_ERR_NEGATIVE` for a negative `Duration`). Overflow is always reported, never wrapped: `musical_time_add` is `MUSICAL_TIME_ERR_OVERFLOW`, `duration_add` and `musical_time_subtract` are `DURATION_ERR_OVERFLOW`, `performance_time_add` and the tempo map are their existing overflow codes. `time/exact_rational.h` is deprecated (no library code includes it) and kept until the Analyzer switches.

## Dependencies

Depends on `identity` (L0) and `core` (L1) only.
