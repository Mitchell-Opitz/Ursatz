# theory

Layer L7 (`music-theory`). Path `src/theory/`, tests `tests/theory/`. Sibling of `rules`; unlike L6, `rules` depends directly on `theory`, but `theory` never depends back on `rules`.

## Provides

`Framework`, `Heuristic`, `Tendency`, `StatisticalModel`, `StyleModel`.

## Notes

- `Framework` holds no reference back into `interpretation`, and carries no forward reference to its own siblings (`Rule`, `Constraint`, `Preference`, `Heuristic`, `Tendency`, `StatisticalModel`, `StyleModel`) either. It is the thing an L6+ consumer resolves a reference against, never the thing that reaches back out. Dependency Spec Open Issue §7.1 is now closed. `Interpretation` carries an optional `FrameworkReference` (`core`, L1) rather than a live reference to this module; see `docs/modules/l6-notes.md`.
- `Tendency`'s frequency is not a Rule/Constraint verdict. `TendencyResult` is a disjoint enum from `RuleResult` and `ConstraintResult`; no function converts a `Tendency` into either, and no function in `rule.h`/`constraint.h` accepts a `Tendency`. An observed frequency (TDD §99) never automatically becomes a must/must-not/valid/invalid claim.
- `StatisticalModel`'s output cannot be silently promoted to a `Rule`. `theory/statistical_model.h` does not include `rules/rule.h`, and no function anywhere converts a `StatisticalModel` into a `Rule`. `training_corpus` is a plain string label (TDD §100), never a corpus object reference.
- `Heuristic` exposes no evaluate/pass-fail API at all. `theory/heuristic.h` is identity plus a label only (TDD §98); nothing on the type could be evaluated as true or false in the first place.
- `StyleModel` is shape only, same as `Tendency` and `StatisticalModel`. `style_features` is an opaque `Value`, `training_corpus` is a plain label string, and there is no fitting or comparison logic. Construction/shape tests only.

## Theory to Corpus open issue (Dependency Spec §7.2)

The System Registry lists `Tendency`, `StatisticalModel`, and `StyleModel` as depending on `Corpus` (L9, not yet implemented). This module does not implement that dependency; the spec is explicit that no implementation may establish it until the resolution is recorded upstream. `Tendency`, `StatisticalModel`, and `StyleModel` each accept corpus-derived statistics only as an opaque, already-computed `Value` supplied by the caller. No file under `src/theory/` or `src/rules/` includes a corpus header. `tests/theory/test_architecture_theory.c` and `tests/rules/test_architecture_rules.c` enforce this by allowlisting only L0-L6 directories.

## Deferred

Species-counterpoint frameworks (TDD Phase 10) and common-practice harmony (Roman numeral analysis, harmonic function, voice leading, cadence, tonicization, modulation, TDD Phase 11) are built as extensions on top of `Framework`/`Rule`/`Constraint` after this module, never as changes to the L7 kernel itself.

The first such extension, `common-practice-minimal-v1`, is now built at `frameworks/common-practice-minimal/` (physically outside `src/`, its own architecture-gate test at the same L0-L6-plus-`Framework`/`Rule`/`Constraint` dependency ceiling `Framework` itself carries). It covers the seven diatonic triads, four diatonic seventh-chord qualities (dominant7, major7, minor7, half-diminished7), and major/natural-minor keys; see that module's own `README.md` for its exact scope and exclusions. This establishes the `frameworks/<name>/` location as the pattern future extensions (jazz harmony, species counterpoint, etc.) are expected to follow.

## Dependencies

Depends on `identity` (L0), `core` (L1), `time`/`pitch` (L2), `events` (L3), `containers` (L4), `structure` (L5), and all four L6 modules only.

A second extension, `standard-notation-v1`, is built at `frameworks/standard-notation/` with the same shape and dependency ceiling. It covers the conventions of Western staff notation: the five-line staff, clef-to-pitch assignment, ledger lines, stem direction, and the drawing of seconds. Its rules are built on the `Clef` and `StaffPosition` value types in `src/pitch/`; see that module's `README.md` for scope and exclusions.

A third notation-adjacent extension, `standard-tonality-v1`, is built at `frameworks/standard-tonality/` with the same shape and ceiling. It covers the tonal conventions of Western music: the major scale (W-W-H-W-W-W-H, named for its first note), the natural, harmonic, and melodic minor scales, scale degrees with movable-do solfege and degree names (including me/le/te and the subtonic), which major or minor key a key signature belongs to, parallel and relative keys, and the circle of fifths with real and imaginary keys and its minor ring. How accidentals are ordered and placed in a key signature is notation and stays in `standard-notation-v1`. Scale-degree vocabulary is a typed set here, while `ScaleDegree` and `Key` in `src/` keep their labels opaque; `standard_tonality_build_scale_degree` builds a populated `ScaleDegree`. It uses 12-TET semitone arithmetic of its own rather than depending on standard-notation. A minor key rests on the natural minor scale; picking the major or minor key from a signature's two candidates is a judgement left to key identification. `common-practice-minimal` is unchanged.

Four more extensions follow the same shape and ceiling. `standard-markings-v1` (`frameworks/standard-markings/`) gives meaning to the Italian marking vocabulary carried by the opaque `Dynamics`, `Articulation`, `TempoMarking`, and `StructuralMarker` types: dynamics order and -issimo stacking, articulation classes, tempo grouping and order, and repeat/ending/D.C./D.S. playback order. The tempo order is the conventional one, asserted as ordinal rank with no bpm ranges, and repeat signs are not retaken after a D.C./D.S. jump unless `take_repeats_after_jump` is set. `standard-rhythm-v1` (`frameworks/standard-rhythm/`) gives `NoteValue` and `Tuplet` their notation meaning: American and British names, the halving hierarchy, notehead fill, stems and flags, rests, dots, ties, and tuplets (triplet 3:2, duplet 2:3, the written number, the span). Ties are checked from plain pitches and values, since the `Tie` entity holds only two NoteEvent ids, and pitch is compared as written. `standard-meter-v1` (`frameworks/standard-meter/`) reads a `TimeSignature` as a simple or compound meter: classification into six categories (an unusual top number such as 5 is unclassified, not invalid), beats per measure, beat division, the dotted compound beat, downbeat and upbeat, measures and multi-measure rests, anacrusis, American counting for both classes, and beaming and beat-boundary ties built on a shared beat grid, borrowed divisions (triplets and duplets, their level and counting), hypermeter, and syncopation. It depends on `time/` types, not on standard-rhythm. Quadruple feeling like two groups of two is perceptual and is not asserted. `western-periods-v1` (`frameworks/western-periods/`) holds the six conventional stylistic periods; their boundaries overlap and a year lookup returns every period containing it.
