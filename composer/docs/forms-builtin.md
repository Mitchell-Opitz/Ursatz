# Built-in forms

The built-in forms and their sizes. Back to [forms.md](forms.md).


`forms/<name>.json`, embedded into the library at build time by `cmake/EmbedForms.cmake` (one byte array for each file in
`COMPOSER_FORM_DOCUMENTS`). `form_spec_builtin_count`, `form_spec_builtin_name`, `form_spec_builtin_document` and
`form_spec_builtin` list and read them in registry order, which is the order of `COMPOSER_FORMS` in `src/CMakeLists.txt` (the last before `game-loop` are `sonatina` and `sonatina-tonic-major`, 22 forms in all).
No built-in names `checks`, so each runs every check. The game loop is the last, and the only one whose sections the
request gives. The minuet, the trio and the minuet and trio come before it (P48, R-487), the scherzo and the scherzo and trio after them (P49, R-492, R-495), the figured variations after those (P50, R-500) the main and subordinate themes after them (P51, R-509) and the Andante and small rondo forms after those (P52, R-520).

| Form | Sizes (default marked) | Sections | Regions and keys |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `binary` | 8, **16** | A, B: presentation + continuation, PAC, half the bars each; B's motive is m0 inverted | `away` V (major) or III (minor): A ends in it, B starts in it. Closing. |
| `rounded-binary` | **16**, 24 | A 8 (presentation + continuation, PAC, closed), B 4 or 8 (compound basic idea + continuation, HC, inverted motive), A' 4 or 8 (returns A, exact, PAC) | `away` as binary. Closing. |
| `ternary` | 12, **24** | The small ternary after Schoenberg ([small-ternary.md](small-ternary.md)): A (antecedent + consequent, PAC, closed), B (presentation + continuation, HC, inverted motive, dominant pedal), A return (returns A, `varied`), a third of the bars each | `away` V or iii (major) or III or v (minor): A ends in it and B starts in it. Closing. |
| `variations` | 16 to 72, count 1 to 8, count **3** | The theme (antecedent + consequent, PAC) and n variations of 8 bars, each returning the theme, every section closed; variation k is `ornament` with a diminished motive (k odd) or `reharmonize` with an interval-changed motive (k even) | `away` vi (major) or III (minor): with three variations or more, the section at index `(count + 1) / 2` (the theme is 0) starts and ends in it. Closing. |
| `loop` | **8**, 16 | 8: one section (presentation + continuation, HC) so the end leads back to the first bar; 16: A (IAC), B (HC, inverted motive) | None. |
| `layered` | 16, 24, **32**, count 2 to 4, count **4** | Layer 1 (presentation + continuation, HC), each later layer returning it, exact. The masks build up: layer 1 the bass, layer 2 the bass and the accompaniment, layer 3 adds the melody, the last layer everything. `accompaniment` is `"required"`. | None. |
| `minuet` | 12, **24** | The minuet after Schoenberg ch. XV ([minuet.md](minuet.md)), 3/4 at 112: A (presentation + continuation, PAC, closed), B (presentation + continuation, HC, inverted motive, dominant pedal, leads into A return, a connective on some seeds), A return (returns A, `varied`), a third of the bars each; `repeats` `[["A"], ["B", "A return"]]`, so it is performed A A B A1 B A1 | `away` V or iii (major) or III or v (minor), as the ternary. Closing. |
| `trio` | 12, **24** | The minuet with a period (antecedent + consequent) for A and A return | `away` as the minuet. Closing. |
| `minuet-and-trio` | 12, **24** | A compound form: the minuet, the trio (a piece of the `trio` form with a motive of its own) and the minuet da capo without its repeats; 120 bars performed at 24 | `trio` IV, vi or the tonic's parallel key (major: IV, vi, the tonic minor; minor: III, VI, the tonic major), drawn for the trio part. Closing. |
| `scherzo` | **44**, 60 | The scherzo after Schoenberg ch. XVI ([scherzo.md](scherzo.md)), 3/4, 2/4 or 6/8 at 176, nine sections: A (presentation + continuation, PAC, closed, 8 bars or 16), P (the pattern, antecedent + consequent, 4 bars), S1 and S2 (return P exact, 4 bars each), L (the liquidation, 4 bars, HC), A return (returns A, `varied`), E (the episode, 4 bars), C1 and C2 (two codettas, 4 bars each); `modulates`, and `repeats` `[["A"], ["P", "S1", "S2", "L", "A return"]]`, so it is performed A A P S1 S2 L A1 P S1 S2 L A1 E C1 C2 (76 bars at 44, 108 at 60) | `pattern` V (major) or v (minor): P's key; `sequence` IV or iv: S1's; `episode` IV or iv: E's. S2, L, A return and the codettas are at home. Closing. |
| `scherzo-and-trio` | **44**, 60 | A compound form, 3/4 at 176: the scherzo, the trio (a piece of the `trio` form of 24 bars, `"bars": 24`, with a motive of its own) and the scherzo da capo without its repeats; 168 bars performed at 44, 216 at 60, in 30 rows | `trio` as the minuet and trio's. Closing. |
| `figured-variations` | 40 (count 3), **56** (count 5) | The set after Schoenberg ch. XVII ([figured-variations.md](figured-variations.md)), 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 and 6/8 (2/4 first), no tempo: the theme (antecedent + consequent, PAC, closed, 8 bars), five variations of 8 bars returning it as `figurate` (V1 neighbor tones in eighths, V2 in sixteenths, V3 a scale in eighths in the parallel mode, V4 a scale in sixteenths over reharmonized chords with the root changed at bar 3, V5 a broken chord in sixteenths, the climax) and two codettas of 4 bars (C1, C2); at 40, three variations (neighbor eighths, parallel scale eighths, climax broken chord) | None: V3 (V2 at 40) is in the home tonic's other mode (`"mode": "parallel"`), the rest at home. Closing. |
| `main-and-subordinate` | 48, **57** | The main and subordinate themes after Schoenberg ch. XVIII ([main-and-subordinate.md](main-and-subordinate.md)), any meter, no tempo: A (presentation + continuation, PAC, closed, 8 bars), T (a transition, 8 bars, HC, ends in the region), S1 (a subordinate theme, 9 bars with `fragment 2` repeated, 8 at 48; motive inverted), S2 (a lyric subordinate theme, an antecedent + consequent period, 8 bars; 57 only), C (a codetta, 4 bars), R (a retransition, 4 bars, HC), A return (returns A `exact`, closed) and K (a coda, 8 bars) | `subordinate` V (major) or III (minor): T's second half, S1, S2 and C. A, R, A return and K are at home. `modulates` and closing. |
| `andante-aba` | **44** | The Andante form ABA after Schoenberg ch. XIX ([rondo.md](rondo.md)), any meter, tempo 72: A (presentation + continuation, PAC, closed, 8 bars), T (a transition, 8 bars, HC), B (a subordinate theme, 8 bars, PAC; motive inverted), R (a retransition, 4 bars), A1 (returns A `embellish`, closed) and K (a coda, 8 bars) | `subordinate` V (major) or III (minor): T's second half and B. A, R, A1 and K are at home. `modulates` and closing. |
| `andante-abab` | **56** | The Andante form ABAB, tempo 72: A T B R A1 as `andante-aba`, then T1 (a transition, 4 bars at home, HC), B1 (returns B `exact`, in the tonic major, `"mode": "major"`) and K | `subordinate` as above; B1 is in the tonic major (the home key in major, the parallel major in minor) and K is at home. `modulates` and closing. |
| `small-rondo-ababa` | **68** | The small rondo ABABA, tempo 88: A T B R A1 T1 B1 as `andante-abab`, then R1 (a retransition, 4 bars), A2 (returns A `setting` `arpeggiated`, closed) and K | `subordinate`, B1 in the tonic major as `andante-abab`; R1, A2 and K are at home. `modulates` and closing. |
| `small-rondo-abaca` | **64** | The small rondo ABACA, tempo 88: A T B R A1 as `andante-aba`, then C (a contrast, 8 bars, antecedent + consequent, PAC, motive `interval-change`), R1, A2 (returns A `setting` `arpeggiated`, closed) and K | `subordinate` as above and `second` vi or IV (major), VI or iv (minor), which C starts and ends in; A, R, A1, R1, A2 and K are at home. `modulates` and closing. |
| `large-rondo` | **88** | The large rondo ABA-C-ABA after Schoenberg ch. XIX ([rondo.md](rondo.md)), tempo 132: A T B R A1 as `andante-aba`, then C (a trio, 8 bars, antecedent + consequent, PAC, motive `interval-change`), R1, A2 (returns A `reharmonize`, closed), T1, B1 (as `andante-abab`), R2 (a retransition, 4 bars), A3 (returns A `setting` `arpeggiated`, closed) and K | `subordinate` as above and `trio` vi or IV (major), VI or iv (minor), which C starts and ends in; A, R, A1, R1, A2, T1, R2, A3 and K are at home. `modulates` and closing. |
| `sonata-rondo` | **88** | The sonata rondo, tempo 132: as `large-rondo` with P (a pattern, 4 bars, antecedent + consequent, IAC) and S (a sequence, 4 bars, returns P `exact`) in place of C | `subordinate` as above, `development` vi (major), iv (minor) for P and `sequence` ii (major), v (minor) for S, each started and ended in; the rest at home as `large-rondo`. `modulates` and closing. |
| `sonatina` | **92** | The sonata-allegro at sonatina size after Schoenberg ch. XX ([sonata.md](sonata.md)), any meter, tempo 132: A, T, B, C1, C2 (the exposition, 32 bars), E (an `elaboration`, B returned `embellish`), P, S, R (20 bars), A1 (returns A `exact`), T1, B1 and C3 (B and C1 returned `reharmonize` in the home mode), C4 and K (40 bars) | `subordinate` V (major) or III (minor) for T's second half, B, C1 and C2; `elaboration` IV (major) or VI (minor) for E; `development` and `sequence` as `sonata-rondo` for P and S; the rest at home. `modulates` and closing. |
| `sonatina-tonic-major` | **92** | As `sonatina` with B1 and C3 returned `exact` in the tonic major (`"mode": "major"`), the music turning minor at C4 in a minor piece | As `sonatina`, B1 and C3 in the tonic major. `modulates` and closing. |
| `game-loop` | none: the request gives the sections | Intro, body, turnaround and ending sections of any length in beats ([The game loop](forms.md#the-game-loop)); `plan_name` is `structure` | None. Not closing. |
