# Changelog archive 2

The Composer changelog entries of phases P51 and P50. Back to the [changelog](changelog.md).

## Unreleased (phase P51)

Rows, forms entry and vocabulary name the section functions and the API is 1.23 (R-510, B8). Each row of a piece (a form with sizes after `figure`, a game loop after `part`) gains `function`: `null`, or `{"kind": "transition" | "retransition" | "subordinate" | "coda", "independent", "lyric", "text"}`; a performed row takes its source section's. The forms entry's sections gain `independent` and `lyric`, the vocabulary gains `piece_functions` (`transition` on `intro` and `body`, `coda` on `ending`, each with `name`, `label` and `parts`), and a game loop request section takes `function` (`null`, `"transition"`, `"coda"`; another value is refused at `<section>/function`). Composed output is unchanged, so the plans and every hash are as before; the `piece-*.stdout.json` snapshots and `piece-variations.result.json` gain `"function": null` on each row and nothing else. The schemas of the piece response (and its variations form), the forms entry, the vocabulary and the piece request gain the members, and the piece response's section `role` lists the four new roles and a row's optional `construction_text` (both were missing). New: `tests/test_api_parts.c`; `test_api_forms` and `test_api_vocabulary` read the new members. See [form-parts.md](form-parts.md), [api/theme-and-piece.md](api/theme-and-piece.md), [api/vocabulary-and-validation.md](api/vocabulary-and-validation.md) and [piece-request.md](piece-request.md).

The main and subordinate themes form is built in (R-509, B7). `forms/main-and-subordinate.json` (label `Main and subordinate themes`, `closing`, `modulates`, any meter, no tempo, the region `subordinate` V or III, sizes 57 (default) and 48) is the 13th built-in form, after `figured-variations` and before `game-loop`: A, T (transition), S1 (subordinate, motive inverted, at 57 bars 9 bars with `fragment 2` repeated), S2 (lyric, 57 only), C (codetta), R (retransition), A return (returns A `exact`) and K (coda). A closing form no longer gives a transition that ends in another key a PAC: `piece_plan_cadences` leaves its half cadence and `piece_check_cadences` accepts it (`piece_cadences.c`); no other form has a transition, so every other plan and output is unchanged (24 new plan lines in `template-plans.txt`). New: [main-and-subordinate.md](main-and-subordinate.md). All 16 measured rows (two sizes, the eight rows of key and meter) compose 10 of 10 and pass the plan's key checks; the return is heard in 148 of 160.

A game loop section may be a transition or a coda (R-508, B6). `PieceSection` gains `function` (`PieceFunction` `PIECE_FUNCTION_NONE`, `_TRANSITION`, `_CODA`; `piece_function_name`, `piece_function_from_name`). `piece_request_check` refuses a transition on the turnaround or the ending and a coda on any part but the ending; `piece_default_cadences` gives a transition without a cadence a half cadence; `add_request_section` refuses a transition that is not a theme section (4 or 8 whole bars on a barline, no pickup, `into` none or sentence, fill empty or motif, no construction, cadence half) and a coda that is not 8 whole bars with no pickup, a perfect authentic cadence and an empty or motif fill, each `PIECE_ERR_BAD_REQUEST`, and gives them the `section_function` constraint (a coda no `archetype`, `theme_pairing` or `free_phrase`). A request without `function` builds the same plan, so every output and the `piece-loop-and-ending.*` snapshots are byte-identical. A loop with a transition and an ending with a coda compose 10 of 10 in C major 4/4 and A minor 3/4 (`test_piece_structure` case `functions`). Documented in [piece-structure.md](piece-structure.md) and [form-parts.md](form-parts.md).

The coda (R-507, B5). New `src/grow/grow_coda.c` (`GROW_CODA_MAGIC`, `grow_coda_applies`, `grow_coda_generate`, `grow_coda_apply`, `grow_coda_judge`, `GrowCodaSpec`), dispatched from `grow-theme` and judged in `grow_checker`: a Constrained childless section with the `coda` function, no `archetype`, `theme_pairing`, `return` or `hold`, a PAC, 8 whole bars at home, is grown as three phrases of 4, 2 and 2 bars, each with a PAC and its own chord targets: m0 exact twice and m0 augmented 2:1 (`m1`; m0 exact when it does not fit 2 bars or the meter cannot write it), then m0 liquidated (`m2`) and one note further (`m3`), then the main theme head (`main theme head`, written as it is) and a held tonic a bar long (`final tone`). `grow_main_theme_head` is the retransition's head made public. The material holds 5 motive bars for it (6 when augmented). No built-in form has a coda, so every output is byte-identical. A 48-bar form with a coda composes 10 of 10 in eight rows of key and meter, every K in three phrases of 4, 2 and 2 bars ending V to I in root position on the tonic, and the plan round-trips (`test_form_parts` case `coda`). Documented in [form-parts.md](form-parts.md).

The retransition (R-505, B4). `grow_liquidate.c` grows a Constrained childless section with the `retransition` function and no `liquidation` constraint as its second layout (`LiquidateRequest.retransition`, `GrowLiquidateSpec.retransition` and `head`): the `basic idea` is the residue (m0 liquidated, 1 to half the notes dropped, a motive node `m1`), the `repetition` the residue one note further (two notes kept at least, else drawn again, `m2`), `fragment 1` a scale line of eighths filling a bar (`scale line`, written as it is), `fragment 2` the broken chord of V (`broken chord`) and the `cadential` idea the main theme head (m0's leading notes that fit half a bar, 2 or 3, `main theme head`, written as it is) on the half cadence; five motive nodes in the material (`RETRANSITION_MOTIVE_BARS` 5). A retransition may start a key change (the key before it is any region: the prolongation of V is the arrival) and refuses one at its half; a liquidation still refuses both. `MAX_RUN` is 4, so the liquidation and the retransition work in 4/4; a four-eighth broken chord of V is 5, 7, 2, 5 so it ends on a tone of V. The scherzo's liquidation in 3/4, 2/4 and 6/8 is byte-identical and no built-in form has a retransition. The liquidation fixture composes 10 of 10 in C major 4/4 and a 48-bar form with a retransition 10 of 10 in eight rows of key and meter (`test_grow_liquidate`, `test_form_parts` case `retransition`). Documented in [grow-liquidation.md](grow-liquidation.md) and [form-parts.md](form-parts.md).

An independent transition has a motive of its own (R-504, B3). `form_build_plan` holds one more bar of material for each section with `independent` and adds an open motive node `independent <section name>` to the material's idea (an `INVENT_MOTIVE` melody span, as m0's), at the first bar after m0's layout and the extensions; the section's `MOTIVE_REF` names it with no step. `GrowThemeRequest` gains `independent`: `read_request` reads a reference with no step to a motive of the material other than m0 as the section's source and clears `has_motive_ref`, and the context's basic motive (m0's) is not used for its chords, features or segments. A form with no independent transition builds and composes as before, so every output is byte-identical; no built-in form has one. A 32-bar form with an independent transition composes 10 of 10 in eight rows of key and meter, its basic idea unlike the theme's, with two invented motives in the material and a plan that round-trips byte for byte (`test_form_parts` case `independent`). Documented in [form-parts.md](form-parts.md) and [forms.md](forms.md).

The transition liquidates and modulates into a dominant (R-504, R-512, B2). `GrowThemeRequest` gains `transition`, set from a `section_function` of `transition`; `grow_generate` then turns `continuation.liquidate` on for that section's request only, so the preferences and every other section are unchanged. `harmony_phrase_propose` deals a second time when `request->pivot` is set and no candidate was kept: the predominant pivots go on to the new key's V7 and I (`deal_progression` takes `confirm`; `MAX_BEFORE_FINAL` is 8), which a half-cadence phrase opening the dominant of a major key needs (its pivots are all predominants and left too few chords), and a phrase that dealt before is dealt the same. A 32-bar form with a transition modulating to V composes 10 of 10 in eight rows of key and meter (`test_form_parts`); `test_harmony_phrase` gains `test_pivot_into_the_dominant`. No built-in form has a transition, so every output is byte-identical. Documented in [form-parts.md](form-parts.md), [grow-continuation.md](grow-continuation.md) and [piece.md](piece.md).

The parts of larger forms are data (R-503, B1). `FormRole` gains `FORM_ROLE_TRANSITION`, `FORM_ROLE_RETRANSITION`, `FORM_ROLE_SUBORDINATE` and `FORM_ROLE_CODA` after `FORM_ROLE_LIQUIDATION` (`FORM_ROLE_COUNT` 18; `transition`, `retransition`, `subordinate`, `coda` in the role table, theme-like), and `FormSectionSpec` gains `independent` and `lyric`, read from the form document (`/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/independent` and `lyric`) and in `schema/form.v1.schema.json`. The reader takes a transition (a presentation or a compound basic idea and a continuation ending in a half cadence, 8 bars when it modulates), a subordinate theme (any theme-like section; `lyric` makes a period whose motive is m0 augmented), a retransition (4 bars, half cadence) and a coda (8 bars, perfect authentic cadence) as the form document describes in [forms.md](forms.md), each refusal at its path. `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_SECTION_FUNCTION` follows `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_FIGURE` with `PlanSectionFunction {function, independent, lyric}` (`PlanFunctionKind` `TRANSITION`, `RETRANSITION`, `SUBORDINATE`, `CODA`; `plan_function_kind_name`, `plan_function_kind_from_name`); plan JSON writes `{"kind": "section_function", "function", "independent", "lyric"}`, every member required, and `schema/plan.v2.schema.json` carries it. `form_build_plan` gives a transition, retransition, subordinate theme and coda the constraint, and a retransition and a coda no `archetype`, `theme_pairing` or antecedent cadence; `compose_run` counts a retransition or coda as a theme section that is not heard, and the style leaves their form alone (`plan_section_function_of` in `plan_util`). A transition and a subordinate theme grow as themes do; a retransition and a coda get their operators in B4 and B5. No built-in form uses a part, so every output is byte-identical. New: `docs/form-parts.md`, `tests/fixtures/forms/parts-of-forms.json`, the reader's refusals in `test_form_spec` and the round trip in `test_plan_json`. See [form-parts.md](form-parts.md), [forms.md](forms.md) and [plan-constraints.md](plan-constraints.md).

## Unreleased (phase P50)

API 1.22 (R-501, B5). A row of a form with sizes gains `figure` after `changes`: `null` for a section without a figure or one that was not copied, else `{"kind", "rhythm", "harmony", "change_at", "climax", "raised", "mode", "skeleton", "text"}` with the skeleton's tones as note names (`form_figure_skeleton`, spelled in the section's key) and the sentence for a person; the `changes.text` of a figurate return says `melody: figured over the skeleton`. The forms entry's sections gain `figure` (`null`, else `kind`, `rhythm`, `harmony`, `change_at`, `climax`) and `mode` (`null` or `"parallel"`). New `util_key_prefers_flats` for the spelling. `piece.response.schema.json`, `piece_variations.response.schema.json` and `forms.schema.json` carry the members. Composed output unchanged: no plan, MIDI, MusicXML, snapshot or hash moved. New: the figure rows of the figured variations and the null figure of a ternary in `test_api_piece`, the figure and mode of the sections in `test_api_forms`. See [api/theme-and-piece.md](api/theme-and-piece.md), [api/vocabulary-and-validation.md](api/vocabulary-and-validation.md) and [figured-variations.md](figured-variations.md).

The figured variations are built in (R-500, B4). `forms/figured-variations.json` (2/4, 3/4, 4/4 and 6/8, 2/4 first, no tempo, `closing`, no regions; the theme, five variations returning it as `figurate` with a figure each, V3 in the parallel mode, V4 over reharmonized chords with the root changed at bar 3, V5 the climax, and the codettas C1 and C2: 56 bars with `count` 5, 40 with `count` 3) is the 12th built-in form, before the game loop. The old `variations` form is unchanged. Plans, MIDI, MusicXML, snapshots and template hashes of every existing form are unchanged (24 new plan lines). `test_piece_keys` counts a change of mode on one tonic as no key change. All 10 measured rows compose 10 of 10; the climax was raised in 0 of 100 pieces and V4's bar 3 changed in 3 of 80 ([figured-variations.md](figured-variations.md)). New: `docs/figured-variations.md`, the form's tests in `test_form_spec`, `test_form_plans`, `test_form_templates` (FORM_TEMPLATES gains it), `test_piece_keys`, `test_api_forms` and `test_api_piece`. See [forms.md](forms.md).

A figurate variation may be in the home key's parallel mode (R-499, B3). `FormSectionSpec` gains `parallel`, read from the form document (`/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/mode`, the one value `"parallel"`; refused without `"variation": "figurate"` and with `keys`, and a figure whose `harmony` is `kept` is refused, the default being `reharmonized`) and in `schema/form.v1.schema.json`. `draw_keys` gives such a section the home tonic in the other mode without a draw; `grow_read_keys` reads no start change on one tonic, `related_to_home` accepts the home tonic and `piece_check_keys` neither counts nor classifies a change between two spans on one tonic, so a change of mode needs no pivot. `return_mode_shift` and `return_copy_change_mode` copy a figurate return across modes (degrees 3 and 6 lowered into minor, 3 and 6 raised and 10 to 11 into major, the leading tone kept), always reharmonized, and `form_figure_skeleton` reads the returned melody mapped the same way. A chord shape reopened in minor no longer keeps its chords inside a bar: the minor phrase model could not deal a half-cadence phrase of four bars otherwise (0 of 10 in C major). No built-in form uses it until B4, so every output is byte-identical. New: `tests/fixtures/forms/parallel-form.json`, `test_form_figure parallel` (10 of 10 in C major 2/4, A minor 2/4, C major 6/8 and A minor 3/4) and the refusals in `test_form_spec`. See [variation-figure.md](variation-figure.md) and [forms.md](forms.md).

The figurate return composes (R-497, R-498, B2). New `form_figure.h` and `src/piece/form_figure.c`: `form_figure_skeleton` (one principal tone per beat cell, a chord tone, `kept` for the last bar of the section and of its first half), `form_figure_write` (N equal notes a cell, the first the skeleton tone: neighbor, scale or broken chord in eighths or sixteenths, the climax an octave up when it fits the range) and `form_figure_check`. `variation_offered` offers `PLAN_RETURN_FIGURATE`: `form-return` copies the section and keeps its melody (a reharmonized figure reopens the harmony as `reharmonize` does, `ReturnPayload.reharmonize`), `form_return_bar_changed` asks whether a chord that starts inside a bar is not the theme's, and `run_returns` writes the figures after the harmony is final, with the reharmonized and `change_at` fit checks in the first half of the attempts. `return_hear` reads a figured section and the section it returns reduced to one note a beat. The cell of the figure is the beat (a quarter, a dotted quarter in 6/8), not the harmony operators' cell, and a broken chord turns back at the range's edge. No built-in form uses the variation until B4, so every output is byte-identical. New: `docs/variation-figure.md`, `tests/test_form_figure.c`. See [variation-figure.md](variation-figure.md).

The figurate return variation and its constraint (R-497, R-498, B1). `PLAN_RETURN_FIGURATE` follows `PLAN_RETURN_VARIED` (`"figurate"` in `plan_return_variation_name`), and `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_FIGURE` follows `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_LIQUIDATION` with `PlanFigure {kind, rhythm, reharmonize, change_at, climax}` (`PlanFigureKind` `NEIGHBOR`, `SCALE`, `BROKEN_CHORD`; `PlanFigureRhythm` `EIGHTHS`, `SIXTEENTHS`; `plan_figure_kind_name`, `plan_figure_kind_from_name`, `plan_figure_rhythm_name`, `plan_figure_rhythm_from_name`); it is valid when the enums are in range and `change_at` is 0 unless `reharmonize`. Plan JSON writes it as `{"kind": "figure", "figure", "rhythm", "harmony": "kept" | "reharmonized", "change_at": <n> | null, "climax"}`, every member required, and `schema/plan.v2.schema.json` carries it. `FormSectionSpec` gains `has_figure` and `figure`, read from the form document (`/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/figure`; refused without the variation `figurate`, with it and no figure, with `beats`, on a liquidation, and a root change over kept harmony or outside the section's bars) and in `schema/form.v1.schema.json`; `form_build_plan` gives such a section the constraint. `variation_offered` does not offer `figurate` until B2, so a figurate section is grown afresh as a return whose keys differ is, and no built-in form uses one until B4: every output is byte-identical. New: `tests/fixtures/forms/figure-form.json`, the figure reader tests in `test_form_spec` and the round trip in `test_plan_json`. See [forms.md](forms.md) and [plan-constraints.md](plan-constraints.md).
