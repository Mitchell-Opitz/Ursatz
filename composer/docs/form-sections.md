# Form sections

The members of a section of a size in a form document. A section is read by the library's section codec (`section_from_json`, use `SECTION_USE_FORM`, [library plan](../../library/docs/modules/plan.md)), which refuses what is about the section itself at its path under the section, and then by `form_section_check` in `src/form/form_spec.c`, which holds the rules that need the form: a name unique in its size, keys that name regions of the form, a return of an earlier section that keeps its construction, the motive grammar, the length and cadence each role has, and the bars a construction adds up to. The role of a transition, retransition, subordinate theme or coda sets the section's function. Back to [forms.md](forms.md).

## A section of a size

| Member | Meaning | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | The section node's name, unique in the size, 1 to 31 characters. | required |
| `letter` | Its form letter, 1 to 3 characters. | required |
| `role` | One of `theme`, `contrast`, `return`, `variation`, `layer`, `pattern`, `sequence`, `episode`, `codetta` (theme-like rows, built as the others are; the names are for the rows and the GUI), `liquidation` (a 4-bar section grown by its own operator, below), `transition`, `retransition`, `subordinate`, `coda` (the parts of larger forms, below), `elaboration` (a theme-like row for the middle section of the sonata-allegro, [sonata.md](sonata.md)), `intro`, `body`, `turnaround`, `ending` (the role table in `form_spec.c`; later phases add rows). | required |
| `bars` or `beats` | Its length, whole bars or whole beats of the meter: exactly one. A section of a sized form must be a theme grow-theme builds: a theme-like role of 4 or 8 whole bars, or of 3 to 16 whole bars with a `construction`; a theme-like section of other bars than 4 or 8 is refused at `bars` without one. Any other section is `PIECE_ERR_BAD_REQUEST` at build. | required |
| `beginning`, `ending` | The theme shape, by the plan names: `presentation`, `antecedent`, `compound_basic_idea`; `continuation`, `consequent`, `cadential` ([grow.md](grow.md)). | required |
| `cadence` | A name of the standard-harmony cadence vocabulary (`harmony_cadence_kind_find`). The plan holds its core type, which must be a PAC, an IAC or an HC. | required |
| `closed` | It may close in the home key with a PAC before the piece ends. | `false` |
| `keys` | `{"start", "end"}`: `"home"` or the name of a region. | both `"home"` |
| `motive` | How its motive is made from m0, as `motive_relation_step_text` writes it (`"invert tonal=1"`, `"diminish"`, `"interval-change"`). | m0 itself |
| `returns`, `variation` | The name of an earlier section it returns, and how it varies it (`exact`, `ornament`, `reharmonize`, `reregister`, `varied`, `embellish` (the ornament melody, eighths split into sixteenths when no longer note can be; R-516), `setting` (the harmony, bass and melody kept, the accompaniment written anew in the section's `setting`; R-518), `figurate` (needs a `figure`; written as [variation-figure.md](variation-figure.md) says). A returning section with no `motive` takes its source's. `variation` needs `returns`. | none; `exact` |
| `setting` | `block`, `arpeggiated`, `alberti` or `oom-pah`: the pattern a `setting` return writes its accompaniment in (R-518). Required with `"variation": "setting"` (`a setting return names its setting` at `/variation`) and refused without it (`only a setting return has a setting` at `/setting`); another name is `unknown setting` at `/setting`. The piece's own accompaniment pattern, and `alberti` in a compound meter, are skipped for the next pattern (see [piece.md](piece.md)). | none |
| `layers` | The lanes that sound in it: `"all"`, or a list of `bass`, `inner_voice`, `melody`. | all, no LAYERS constraint |
| `construction` | A theme-like section of whole bars is an uneven theme (R-468): `"auto"`, or `{"ideas": {"<idea name>": bars}, "extensions": [{"kind", "idea", "bars", "varied"}]}` as the [theme request](theme-request.md) gives it. The section is 3 to 16 bars (`bars`), and with an object its ideas and extensions add up to them (`/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/construction`). `"auto"` has the Composer choose the construction at build from the section's bars and the piece's motive ([grow-construction.md](grow-construction.md)); the build is `PIECE_ERR_BAD_REQUEST` when none fits. The ideas must be the pairing's (checked at build, with the meter). A returning section copies its source, so it keeps the source's construction: `a return keeps the construction of <source>` at `/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/construction`. A section with one has its construction constraint and its material gets the bars of its repeats and addition. Only the scherzo's A and A return at 60 bars use it among the built-in forms ([scherzo.md](scherzo.md)); [tests/fixtures/forms/uneven-form.json](../tests/fixtures/forms/uneven-form.json) is a 20-bar form of a 4+6 period and a 4+6 sentence with an evaded cadence. | none, no CONSTRUCTION constraint |
| `pedal` | `"dominant"`: its presentation is written over a pedal on the dominant (R-446). Refused for any other value, and for a section whose `beginning` is not `presentation` (`/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/pedal`). The section gets a `pedal` constraint; grow keeps it only when the section starts in the home key with no Given material in its first half. The B of the ternary, the minuet and the trio has it. | none, no PEDAL constraint |
| `leads_in` | `true`: the section is held until the sections after it are written, then filled against them (R-486, [form-performance.md](form-performance.md)). Refused on the last section of a size, on a section that returns another, on a section another returns and on one whose next section also leads in (`/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/leads_in`). The section gets the `hold` constraint. The B of the minuet and the trio uses it ([minuet.md](minuet.md)); [tests/fixtures/forms/lead-form.json](../tests/fixtures/forms/lead-form.json) composes in C major 3/4 (10 of 10 seeds, connective written in 7) and A minor 3/4 (10 of 10, written in 6). | false |
| `connective` | `"sometimes"` or `"always"`: the held section ends with a stepwise link into the next section's first note (see [form-performance.md](form-performance.md)). Refused without `leads_in` (`/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/connective`). The section gets a `connective` constraint, on every seed or on some. | none, no CONNECTIVE constraint |
| `figure` | `{"kind": "neighbor" \| "scale" \| "broken-chord", "rhythm": "eighths" \| "sixteenths", "harmony": "kept" \| "reharmonized", "change_at": <bar>, "climax": <bool>}`: the motive of variation of a `figurate` return (R-497, R-498); `kind` and `rhythm` are required, `harmony` is `kept` by default, `change_at` is a bar from 1 before the section's last and needs `reharmonized`, `climax` is false by default. Refused without `"variation": "figurate"` (`/figure`), with `figurate` and no figure (`/variation`), with `beats` (`/figure`) and on a liquidation; a root change over kept harmony or outside the bars (`/figure/change_at`). The section gets a `figure` constraint. Only the `figured-variations` form has one among the built-in forms ([figured-variations.md](figured-variations.md)); [figure-form.json](../tests/fixtures/forms/figure-form.json) is the test form. | none |
| `mode` | `"parallel"`: the section is in the home key's parallel key (the same tonic, the other mode; R-499, [variation-figure.md](variation-figure.md)). Only a figurate variation: refused otherwise and with `keys` (`/mode`); its figure is `reharmonized` by default and `"harmony": "kept"` is refused (`/figure/harmony`). The section gets the parallel key in its key lane span and `key` constraint, with no pivot. Only the `figured-variations` form has one among the built-in forms (its V3, [figured-variations.md](figured-variations.md)); [parallel-form.json](../tests/fixtures/forms/parallel-form.json) is the test form. `"major"`: the section is in the home tonic's major key (R-517): home in a major piece, the parallel major in a minor one (B returns in A major in an A minor piece). Only a returning section: refused with `keys` (`a section in the tonic major has no keys`), without `returns` (`the tonic major is for a returning section`) and with a figurate variation (`a figurate variation takes the parallel mode, not the tonic major`), each at `/mode`. The section gets the major key in its key lane span and `key` constraint with no draw, so no other form's draws move; the return operator transposes major to major, the key check takes the parallel key as related (R-499) and the next section opens home with a change of mode on one tonic and no pivot. The returned section's source must be in a major key: a source in a minor key cannot be transposed into major, so such a form builds and its return is not made (the run is stuck at the returns). No built-in form has one yet. `"home"`: the section is at home in the home mode (R-524), its source copied across modes when it is in the other: in a minor piece B returns in the tonic minor, not in III. Only a returning section whose variation is `reharmonize`: refused with `keys` (`a section in the home mode has no keys`), without `returns` (`the home mode is for a returning section`) and with any other variation (`a return in the home mode is reharmonized`), each at `/mode`. The section's RETURN gets `across_modes`, so a source in the other mode is moved by the tonic step and mapped degree by degree into the target's mode (the minor's degrees 4 and 9 semitones above the tonic lowered by a semitone) and reharmonized; a source in the same mode is moved as any return is, so in a major piece it is B moved from V to I. Without it no `reharmonize` return crosses modes, so no existing form changes. [tests/test_form_return.c](../tests/test_form_return.c) `home` is the test form. | none |
| `role` `liquidation` | A section of 4 bars at home over a dominant pedal that liquidates the pattern (R-494, [grow-liquidation.md](grow-liquidation.md)). It has no `beginning` or `ending` (`a liquidation has no beginning or ending`, at their paths), and refuses `construction`, `motive`, `returns`, `variation`, `pedal`, `leads_in`, `connective`, `layers` and `beats` (each at its path); `bars` is 4 (`a liquidation is 4 bars` at `/bars`) and `cadence` is `half` (`/cadence`). The section gets a `liquidation` constraint in place of `archetype` and `theme_pairing`. The scherzo's fifth section is one ([scherzo.md](scherzo.md)). | none |
| `independent` | `true`: a transition made from a motive of its own (R-504, [form-parts.md](form-parts.md)). Read only for a transition (`only a transition is independent` at `/independent` otherwise) and refused with `motive` (`an independent transition has its own motive` at `/motive`). The section's `section_function` constraint carries it, and the plan gets a motive node `independent <section name>` in the material that the section's motive reference names, so the section's ideas come from it, not from m0. | `false` |
| `lyric` | `true`: a subordinate theme whose motive is m0 augmented (R-506). Read only for a subordinate theme (`only a subordinate theme is lyric` at `/lyric` otherwise); refused with `motive` (`a lyric theme's motive is m0 augmented`) or `returns` (`a lyric theme returns nothing`), and its pairing must be antecedent + consequent (`a lyric theme is a period` at `/beginning`). The reader sets its motive to `augment` with an open ratio. | `false` |
| `role` `transition` | A theme-like section (R-503, [form-parts.md](form-parts.md)): `beginning` `presentation` or `compound_basic_idea` (`a transition begins with a presentation or a compound basic idea`), `ending` `continuation` (`a transition ends with a continuation`), `cadence` `half` (`a transition ends in a half cadence`), no `returns`, `variation`, `figure`, `mode` or `pedal` (`a transition has no <member>`); 8 bars when `keys.end` differs from `keys.start` (`a transition that modulates is 8 bars` at `/bars`), else 4 or 8, or 3 to 16 with a `construction`. The section gets a `section_function` constraint. | none |
| `role` `subordinate` | A theme-like section with any pairing, cadence, keys and construction a theme takes, and the `lyric` member. The section gets a `section_function` constraint. | none |
| `role` `retransition`, `coda` | A section grown by its own operator, with no `beginning` or `ending` (`a retransition has no beginning or ending`) and none of `construction`, `motive`, `returns`, `variation`, `pedal`, `leads_in`, `connective`, `layers`, `beats`, `figure`, `mode`, `keys`, `independent`, `lyric` (`a retransition has no keys`, ... at the member's path). A retransition is 4 bars (`a retransition is 4 bars`) ending `half` (`a retransition ends in a half cadence`); a coda is 8 bars (`a coda is 8 bars`) ending `perfect-authentic` (`a coda ends in a perfect authentic cadence`). The section gets a `section_function` constraint in place of `archetype` and `theme_pairing`. | none |

The reader refuses anything it does not know, naming the member by its JSON pointer (`FormSpecError.path`, for example
`/sizes/0/sections/2/returns`): an unknown member at any level, a wrong type, a missing required member, `schema` or
`version` not as above, a name outside `[a-z0-9-]{1,31}`, an unknown role, beginning, ending, cadence name, region,
variation, check or lane, a cadence whose core is not a PAC, an IAC or an HC, `bars` and `beats` together or neither, a
section name repeated in a size, a `construction` that is not `"auto"` or an object of ideas, that adds up to other bars than the section's, or on a section that is not a theme-like role of whole bars, a theme-like section of 3 to 16 bars other than 4 or 8 without one, `returns` naming a later or unknown section, a returning section whose `construction` is not its source's, `variation` without `returns`, two default
sizes or none, a size's `bars` not the sum of its sections, two sizes of the same `bars` and `count`, and more sizes,
sections or regions than the limits (`FORM_SPEC_MAX_SIZES`, `FORM_SPEC_MAX_SECTIONS`, `FORM_SPEC_MAX_REGIONS`). For a form
whose sections the request gives it also refuses `roles` that are not the four parts in order (`/roles/<i>`) or have no
body, a `loop` or cadence rule role that is not one of the form's `roles` (`/loop/roles/<i>`,
`/cadences/last/<i>/roles/<j>`), a missing `roles`, `loop` or `cadences`, and `roles`, `loop`, `cadences` or
`default_ending` in a form that has sizes.
