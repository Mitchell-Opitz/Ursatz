# Changelog

The Composer versions and what each changed. Back to the [docs index](README.md). Older entries are in the [changelog archive](changelog-archive.md).

## Unreleased (phase P47)

A theme may be uneven (R-468, R-471, B2). `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_CONSTRUCTION` (`"construction"` in the plan JSON and its schema) on a theme section gives the bars of each idea (`PlanConstruction`, `idea_bars` by `GrowIdea`, up to 3 extensions, a reason), and `GrowRequest.construction` adds it to the theme section. With one a theme is 3 to 16 bars, 4 or 8 without (`/bars` is refused otherwise), and `grow_layout_of` lays its ideas end to end in each half from the bars it gives (a construction with an extension is not laid out until B3). `grow_construction_buildable` and the refusals at `/construction/ideas` (the ideas are not the theme's bars, an idea the pairing does not have, an idea with no bar, a continuation fragment with as many bars as the basic idea) are beside `grow_theme_buildable`; each half's bars set its room for the cadence, the cadence bar of an antecedent, and the rooms of idea and slot motives. `grow_motive_fills_part` and `grow_part_basic_idea` take the part's and the basic idea's bars. A continuation of whole-bar fragments speeds its harmony when asked (`faster_harmony` reads the fragment's length, not 8 bars), and the label of an uneven theme names the halves (`sentence 4+5 bars`). With a construction a basic idea of 3 bars or any length outside 1, 2 and 4 only lowers the score (`judge_basic_idea`), and a contrasting idea shorter than its motive is made from the leading notes of that motive that fit (`grow_shorten_source`, the material node `shortened for m<place>`). Without a construction every plan, hash fixture, snapshot and recorded rate is byte-identical. New: `test_grow_uneven` (a 3+3, 5+5 and 4+6 period and a 4+5, 5+4 and 6+5 sentence compose 10 of 10 seeds in C major 4/4 and A minor 3/4; the refusals; the plan round trip). See [grow-construction.md](grow-construction.md).

## Unreleased (phase L3)

The segments motif generator can syncopate (R-454, B1). `MotiveRandomPreferences` gains `syncopation_one_in` (0 never, and no random number is drawn), `syncopation_types` (off_beat, tie_beat, tie_bar, anticipation) and `syncopation_levels` (eighth, sixteenth), set from C only: the preferences document, vocabulary, schemas and API version come in B6, so no API output moved. A cell's figure (`off_beat`, `tie_beat`) goes at a drawn beat through `tile`; a boundary figure (`tie_bar`, `anticipation`) moves a sixteenth or an eighth between the last note before a segment and its first, so note counts and the a' mapping do not change, and the chord stays on the segment's beat (`Layout.start`). A note held across a beat is a chord tone, a tied note is a chord tone of both chords, and an early note is read like a run note by `tones_as_drawn`. Simple meters only, never the last note, and the first 16 of a cell's 32 tries may syncopate, the last 16 may not. With the defaults every motive is byte-identical. New: `syncopation_figures`, `syncopation_boundaries` and `syncopation_changes_nothing_else` in `test_motif_preferences`. See [cli-random-motive.md](cli-random-motive.md).

The segments motif generator limits skips apart from leaps (R-455, B2). `MotiveRandomPreferences` gains `skips_per_motif` (the most intervals of 3 or 4 semitones in a motive; 11, the default, never rejects), set from C only: the preferences document, vocabulary, schemas and API version follow in B6. A step (1 or 2 semitones) and a repeated note are neither skips nor leaps, and `leaps_per_motif` and `skips_per_motif` both count every adjacent interval (pickup, non-chord tones and segment joins). No random number is drawn, so with the default every composed output is byte-identical.

The segments motif generator takes a chord plan as written (R-456 to R-461, B3). `MotiveRandomPreferences` gains `plan_count` (0, the default, draws the plan as before) and `plan` (up to `MOTIVE_RANDOM_PLAN_MAX` = 4 `MotiveRandomPlanChord {degree, bar, beat}`), set from C only: the preferences document, vocabulary, schemas and API version follow in B6, so no API output moved. Every diatonic triad of the key is a chord (`chord_shift` covers degrees 1 to 7; in minor only V's third is raised, so the minor set is i ii° III iv V VI VII). A `Layout` now holds the chord at each note (`degree[]`, `chord_first[]`, from `chord_degree[]` and `chord_onset[]` by `set_chord_list` and `set_note_chords`) and every melody step reads it instead of the segment's plan degree; the chords of the motive and `tones_as_drawn` read the same list. Unplaced chords go one to a segment, so the count fixes the shape (`shape_weights`); placed ones (bar and beat, the first at 1:1 holding the pickup) draw the shape as usual and the first note on each is a chord tone. `motive_random_segments_plan_fits` refuses a plan that never fits (`MOTIVE_RANDOM_ERR_PLAN`, `"plan"`) before anything is drawn, so no seed is spent. The open-ending test reads the last chord's tones (not IV, and now not vi). With no plan every motive is byte-identical. New: `given_plans` and `plans_that_do_not_fit` in `test_motif_preferences`. See [cli-random-motive.md](cli-random-motive.md).

The continuation accelerates when asked (R-462 to R-464, B4). `GrowContinuation` (`grow.h`) has `every_segment`, `fragment_lengths` (segment, half_bar, beat), `diminish` and `liquidate`, carried in `GrowContext.continuation` and `GrowSetup.continuation` and set from C only: the preferences document, vocabulary, schemas and API version come in B6, so no API output moved. With any set, `grow_ending.c` draws the continuation by `draw_accelerating_continuation`: fragments from every segment, a fragment cut to its leading notes within half a bar or a beat (a `fragment` leading step on the segment), a whole-segment fragment in diminution 1:2 (a `diminish` step), and a liquidated fragment 2 with the cadential idea a step further. A cut or diminution the library refuses or that does not fit is the whole segment (3/4 keeps its one-bar segments whole). `grow_ref_departs` treats every `fragment` ref as departing from its source, so a cut is ranked and never refused, and `judge_continuation` measures a liquidated fragment against m0. `set_continuation_harmony` is the continuation's harmonic rhythm, moved out of `draw_continuation` unchanged. With none set every output is byte-identical. New: `test_grow_continuation` (slow, with `test_grow_continuation_rates` and `test_grow_continuation_smoke`) and the fixtures `tests/fixtures/continuation/s77.json` and `u51.json`. See [grow-continuation.md](grow-continuation.md).

The continuation changes chord every cell and a motif stands as written when asked (R-465, R-466, B5). `GrowContinuation` gains `faster_harmony` and `GrowContext` and `GrowSetup` gain `basic_as_written`, set from C only: the preferences document, vocabulary, schemas and API version come in B6, so no API output moved. `faster_harmony`: in an 8-bar sentence's continuation with no key change and two cells or more a bar (4/4, 6/8), `build_parts` writes one harmony target an idea (`add_faster_targets`), each of at most one cell and none across a barline: the fragments prolong the tonic to the dominant and the cadential idea is the cadential progression with the cadence; in 2/4 and 3/4 nothing changes. `basic_as_written`: a basic motive with chords as long as the basic idea (no part-length motif, section motive, given first half or pedal) is the basic idea as it is: `build_presentation` ends its harmony target on any chord, its melody is `grow_add_melody_written` (one Realized span of m0's notes) and the sequence guard reads the compose run's basic motive (`GrowThemeSpec.basic_has_chords`); the guard itself is left as it was for every other request (R-466). With both off every output is byte-identical. New: `faster_harmony_changes_each_cell` and `basic_as_written_keeps_the_motif` in `test_grow_continuation`, both settings in its rates, and the fixtures `s77-chords.json` and `u51-chords.json`. See [grow-continuation.md](grow-continuation.md) and [grow-harmony.md](grow-harmony.md).

The preferences document carries the L3 settings and the API is 1.17 (R-422, R-453, B6). The `motif` group reads, writes and checks `syncopation_one_in` (0 to 64), `syncopation_types`, `syncopation_levels`, `skips_per_motif` (0 to 11) and `plan` (1 to 4 Roman numerals with an optional `@bar` or `@bar:beat`, written back upper case and not written when absent), after `long_nct_lengths`; the `development` group reads, writes and checks `continuation` (`every_segment`, `fragment_lengths`, `diminish`, `liquidate`, `faster_harmony`) and `basic_as_written`, after `fidelity_minimum`, and `ComposerPreferences` gains `continuation` and `basic_as_written`. A theme request passes them to the grow run. A random motive run answers `MOTIVE_RANDOM_ERR_PLAN` with `COMPOSER_ERR_REQUEST` at `/preferences/motif/plan`; `validate` leaves that meter-dependent refusal to the run. The vocabulary's `limits.preferences` gains `syncopation_one_in_max`, `skips_per_motif_max`, `plan_chords_max` and `plan_beat_max`, and the random motive, theme and vocabulary schemas gain the members. Every member is off or at its default, so a request without them answers as before and no motive, snapshot or baseline moved; the default document is longer. The motif group's table moves to [preferences-motif.md](preferences-motif.md) (`preferences.md` passed 20 KB). New: tests for every setting read back through write and read, every refusal with its path, the four limits, the plan refusal in a random motive run, and `listen` runs with every L3 setting on. See [preferences.md](preferences.md) and [api.md](api.md).

## Unreleased (phase P46)

The piece rows say what the Analyzer heard and how a return differs (R-449, B5, API 1.16). A row of a form with sizes gains, after `motive`: `key_heard` (the key region heard at the section's start over the whole piece, `"C major"`, or `null`), `pedal` (`"dominant"` when a harmony span in the section's first half was written with a `pedal` shape) and `changes` (`null` unless the section returns one; else `{"copied", "variation", "melody_added", "chords_changed", "chords", "cadence_from", "cadence_to", "text"}`, with `text` such as `"melody: 3 notes added; harmony: 4 of 12 chords changed; cadence: ii6 V7 I for IV V I"` or `"composed anew from A's shape and motive (its keys differ)"`; the reason reads `its length differs` when the lengths differ, as in the rounded binary's shorter A'). `heard` without a check is now the last cadence the compose run heard whose arrival is in the section's last bar; with a check it is as before. `form_hear_keys` (`form_hear.h`) hears the key regions of a span, `form_return_copies` and `form_return_cadence_numerals` (`form_return.h`) tell whether a section holds a copy and what its last quarter's chords are, and `form_return_source` shares its geometry with the first. The forms entry's section gains `pedal`: `null` or `{"value": "dominant", "label": "Dominant pedal"}`. The API is 1.16; the game loop rows, every composed plan, MIDI and MusicXML are unchanged. The three schemas that hold a sized row or the forms entry gain the members. New: `test_form_hear` (the key regions of the C major and the C to G sentences), the ternary rows in `test_api_piece` (B heard HC and on a pedal, A's key, A return `varied` with melody added, a reconstructed A return with `modulate`) and `test_pedal` in `test_api_forms`. See [api/theme-and-piece.md](api/theme-and-piece.md), [form-hear.md](form-hear.md) and [small-ternary.md](small-ternary.md).

The ternary form is the small ternary of Schoenberg ch. XIII (R-445, B4). `forms/ternary.json` has A (a period, PAC, closed, ending in the `away` region with `modulate`), B (presentation + continuation ending on V with a half cadence, inverted motive, `"pedal": "dominant"`, starting in the `away` region with `modulate`) and A return (returns A `varied`); the `away` region is V or iii in major and III or v in minor (it was IV, vi or V and III, iv or VI). Sizes stay 12 and 24. Only ternary plans and outputs moved: the ternary lines of `template-plans.txt`, the ternary expectations in `test_form_spec`, `test_form_templates`, `test_form_return`, `test_plan_cli` and `test_return_heard`, and the baselines (`BASELINES` ternary 39, 117, 51; ternary returns heard 9 of 10). New: [small-ternary.md](small-ternary.md), with the table measured with the real `varied` return (10 of 10 compose in every row). Every other form's plans, MIDI, MusicXML, snapshots and rates are byte-identical.

A returning section may be `varied` (R-447, B3): a moderately varied repetition as Schoenberg describes A1. `PLAN_RETURN_VARIED` is a fifth return variation, named `varied` in the form document, the plan JSON and the form schema. The names come from one table (`plan_return_variation_name`, `plan_return_variation_from_name`) instead of four copies. `form_vary.c` makes it with `ornament_melody` limited to one added note a bar, then `reharmonize`, and checks it with `check_varied` (every note of the returned melody kept at its offset, added notes off the strong beats, passing or neighbor, at most one a bar and at least one in all, the other lanes open); the per-note ornament test is shared with `ornament`. `form_return_cadence_changed` tells whether a chord of the section's last quarter differs from the returned one's, and in the first half of the 12 attempts a varied return needs it. No built-in form uses `varied` until B4, so no plan, MIDI, MusicXML, snapshot or rate moved. New: `test_form_return varied` (`tests/fixtures/forms/varied-form.json`, 10 of 10 seeds compose, a chord of the last 2 bars differs in 8). See [piece.md](piece.md), [forms.md](forms.md) and [plan-constraints.md](plan-constraints.md).

A form section may ask for a dominant pedal (R-446, B2). New plan constraint `pedal` (`PLAN_CONSTRAINT_PEDAL`, `degree` 5 only) and a `pedal` member of `PlanHarmonyShape` (only with `starts_on` and `ends_on` dominant on a prolongation), written to the plan JSON only when set. `harmony_prolong.c` has a second table, `PEDAL_FIGURES` (V, V-I6/4-V, V-I6/4-V7, V-V7), drawn from only by a shape with `pedal`; the root-position-V-off-the-downbeat rule applies only to a figure that starts on the tonic. grow-theme keeps a `pedal` constraint only for a presentation that starts in the home key with no Given material in its first half, gives both ideas the pedal shape and never draws a sequence for them. The form document reads the section member `"pedal": "dominant"` (a presentation only) and `form_build.c` adds the constraint. No built-in form uses it until B4, so no plan, MIDI, MusicXML, snapshot or rate moved. New: tests for the constraint, the figures, the member and its refusals, and `test_form_spec pedal` (`tests/fixtures/forms/pedal-form.json`, 10 of 10 seeds compose with B's first half on the dominant). See [forms.md](forms.md), [grow-harmony.md](grow-harmony.md) and [plan-constraints.md](plan-constraints.md).

Given material with no form goes in the B of a binary form (R-448, B1). The default form of a plan with `--given-*` material and no `--form` is a binary form of 16 bars instead of a ternary form of 24 (`DEFAULT_GIVEN_BARS` 16, `check_request`, the `plan` help and its `no --form:` line), because the ternary form's B will end on the dominant (B4). The tests that place a given melody in a ternary B move to binary, the `test_middle_out` melody baseline is `{10, 19, 18}` (20 sections a run set), and the `plan-given`, `fill-given` and `harmonize` snapshots are recorded again. No other snapshot, plan hash or rate moved.

## Unreleased (phase L2)

Triplets run through the motive texts and themes (R-435, R-442, B3). The Ursatz and Analyzer pins move to the merges of the triplet motive times (`49a5523`, `4374060`). A duration token is a letter then up to two dots or a `3`, a triplet value two thirds of the plain one (`q3` 1/6, `e3` 1/12, `s3` 1/24; `q.3` is refused): `motive_notes.c` reads it and `motive_text.c`, `motive_analysis.c` and `api_develop.c` write it for a time whose denominator is divisible by 3, so every other text is unchanged. `grow_check.c` counts fragments in 384 ticks per whole note, and a theme that scales the rhythm of a motive with triplets passes over a ratio whose result has none (`grow_theme.c`). `test_form_plans` masks the pinned Ursatz commit before it hashes a plan, so a pin bump no longer moves the pinned form plans (their fixtures were recorded once more). New: `test_grow_triplets` (slow, with `test_grow_triplets_smoke`), a `listen` case for a triplet motif. Binary motives and every snapshot are as before. See [motive-format.md](motive-format.md#triplet-values-in-the-texts) and [theme-request.md](theme-request.md).

The segments generator draws rhythm from a tick grid (R-439, B4). Segment motives count time in twelfths of the beat unit (`Grid`, `TICKS_PER_UNIT`, `Rhythm.ticks_per_unit`; the drawn strategy still counts half-beats), and `MotiveRandomPreferences.durations` has eight entries: after `half_beat`, `beat` and `two_beats` come `quarter_beats`, `long_short`, `short_long`, `triplet` and `triplet_two_beats`, all 0 by default. Notes between two frame notes are filled as a run by step (`draw_run`, `Melody.run`) and `tones_as_drawn` reads a run note as a chord tone or a resolved non-chord tone. `compound_beats` tiles 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8 in dotted beats with a dotted-eighth, sixteenth, eighth feature. The settings are set from C only: the preferences document reads the new durations but does not write them, and the vocabulary, schemas and API version come in B6. With the defaults every motive, plan, file and snapshot is byte-identical. `test_motif_preferences` covers each figure, the compound grid and the count of seeds that still give a motive. See [cli-random-motive.md](cli-random-motive.md).

The segments generator ends motives and places long non-chord tones as asked (R-440, R-441, B5). `MotiveRandomPreferences` gains `endings` (free, closed, open, lead_in), `long_nct_one_in`, `long_nct_types`, `long_nct_intervals`, `long_nct_beats` and `long_nct_lengths`, free and 0 by default, so with none set every motive, file and snapshot is byte-identical. The ending is drawn once after the plan only when closed, open or lead_in has a weight; `Grid` carries it with `last_offset`, and a closed ending draws the last note's start among the beats of the highest metric level (`closed_start`). `ending_holds` checks the last note of a closed or open ending, and a lead-in's last note is a frame note. `place_long_accents` runs before `place_accents` and marks the segments it fills, `motive_random_forms` knows the retardation, and they are set from C only until the preferences document carries them (B6).

The preferences document carries the L2 settings and the API is 1.15 (R-422, B6). The `motif` group reads and writes the five new `durations` (the writer no longer stops at the first three), `compound_beats`, `endings`, `long_nct_one_in` (0 to 64, `COMPOSER_PREFERENCES_LONG_NCT_ONE_IN_MAX`) and the four `long_nct_*` lists, after `final_long` in the document's order; a list of all zeros, a `long_nct_one_in` above 64, a non-boolean `compound_beats` and an unknown member are refused with their paths. The vocabulary's `limits.preferences` gains `long_nct_one_in_max`, and the random motive, theme and vocabulary schemas gain the members. All are off or at their defaults, so a request without them answers as before and no motive, snapshot or baseline moved; the default document is longer. New: tests for every setting read back through write and read, the refusals, the limit, a validate refusal and a `listen` run with every L2 setting on in 4/4 and 6/8. See [preferences.md](preferences.md).

## Unreleased (phase P45)

The API serves the forms (R-428, R-430, R-433, B1): `composer_api_forms` gives the seven built-in forms in registry order, each with its label, versions, settings, regions and, for every size, its sections (role, length, beginning and ending, cadence, keys, motive, return, layers and the slots of its theme with their ideas), and, for the game loop, its roles, loop roles, cadences and default ending. The vocabulary's `theme_slots` entries gain `required` (the basic slot) and its `piece_intos` entries gain `beginning` and `ending` where the archetype is one pairing, and the API is 1.13. `form_role_label` labels the roles, `api_piece_version_names` names the versions of a piece, and the API's allocation failure and JSON add helpers are shared (`api_fail_alloc`, `api_add`). A piece still composes only as the game loop and no output, snapshot or baseline moved. The changelog from P37 back is in [changelog-archive.md](changelog-archive.md) and the compose rates are in [grow-hearing.md](grow-hearing.md#compose-rates), so the docs are under their size limit again. See [api/vocabulary-and-validation.md](api/vocabulary-and-validation.md).

The piece entries compose any form (R-429, B2): `composer_api_piece` and `composer_api_piece_variations` read a piece request of `schema_version` 2 with `form`, `bars`, `count` and `modulate` (version 1 stays the game loop and refuses a `form`), compose a form with sizes as the one version `piece` (the plan is built again with each attempt's seed, so a modulating piece plans its keys again), write one of its sections again by its name, and vary it. The result names its versions by the form (`versions`, `plans`, a variation's entry), lists the sections of the size with their role, return and motive, and `musicxml_versions` gives the MusicXML of the later versions. `PieceMade` holds its versions by index, `describe_sizes` is `api_form_sizes_text`, `composer_api_validate` fills the size of a request with sizes, the `piece` command writes the first version and refuses an ending option for a form with none, the piece schemas describe both shapes, and the API is 1.14. A game loop request, version 1 or 2, answers byte-identically and no snapshot, plan, baseline or rate moved. See [piece-request.md](piece-request.md#a-piece-of-another-form-schema-version-2).

## Unreleased (phase P44)

Stuck form runs are fixed at their two causes (R-418, B6). A minor half-cadence continuation or free phrase gets chords that fill it: `grow_chords_before_last` (`grow_theme.c`) counts the chords before the last, `draw_continuation` takes its cap and its split from it, and a free phrase's cap is at least what it needs. The key check hears the part of a section after a key change inside it from the change, in its new key (`piece_keys.c`), so a modulation inside a section is no longer downgraded to a tonicization. Every template completes 20 of 20 runs in `test_piece_keys` and the five `WILL_FAIL` are removed; the form baselines rise to binary 30, 60, 20, rounded binary 40, 120, 58, ternary 30, 90, 35, variations 60, 220, 184, loop 40, 60, 32 and layered 60, 180, 74. A game loop in D minor 4/4 with a free phrase ending HC composes. Runs that completed before give the same output and no snapshot moved. New: `test_grow_half_cadence`, `test_piece_structure minor_half_free`. Known limits are in [piece.md](piece.md).

The game loop is a form (R-419, B5): `forms/game-loop.json` is the last built-in form, with `"sections": "request"`, the roles `intro`, `body`, `turnaround` and `ending`, the loop of the body and the turnaround, the cadences a section with none gets and the default ending (the reader has these members now, `form_spec.h`, `schema/form.v1.schema.json`). `form_build_plan` builds it from `PieceRequest.sections` (`form_build_request.c`), `piece_build_ending` builds the ending version, and `piece_request_check`, `piece_default_cadences` and `piece_default_ending` replace the structure's checks and defaults. `PieceSection`, `PiecePart` and `PieceFillKind` moved to `piece.h`, and the helpers a regenerated piece needs to `piece_reopen.h`. `PieceStructure`, `piece_structure_build_plan`, `piece_ending_plan` and `piece_structure.h` are removed, and `docs/piece-structure.md` is rewritten for the form, with the document's members in [forms.md](forms.md#the-game-loop). The API builds every piece through `piece_build_plan` and `piece_build_ending`: requests, responses and errors are as they were. Every plan stays byte-identical: `test_form_plans_pieces` pins the loop plan of the four piece request fixtures, and no snapshot, baseline or fixture changed. See [forms.md](forms.md).

The forms are data (R-416, B4): the six form templates are JSON documents in `forms/` (binary, rounded binary, ternary, variations, loop, layered), embedded at build time by `cmake/EmbedForms.cmake` and read by `form_spec_parse` (`form_spec.h`, `schema/form.v1.schema.json`). One builder, `form_build_plan` (`form_build.c`, renamed from `piece_request.c`), makes the plan of every form; `piece_build_plan` finds the built-in form named by `PieceRequest.form`, which is now a `const char *`. The sections, sizes, cadences, returns, motives and layers, the keys a piece may move to (`regions` and each section's `keys`) and which sections close in the home key come from the document: `piece_plan_keys` and `piece_plan_cadences` take the form and the size, `piece_check_cadences` takes a form (NULL: the built-in the plan is named for) and `ComposeConfig.form` names the form whose `checks` `compose_run` makes. `form_template.h`, `form_template.c` and the `FormTemplateKind` enum are removed. A form added as a document alone composes (`tests/fixtures/forms/bar-form.json`).
