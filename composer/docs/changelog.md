# Changelog

The Composer versions and what each changed. Back to the [docs index](README.md). Older entries are in the [changelog archive](changelog-archive.md).

## Unreleased (phase P49)

A form may always modulate, and four theme-like roles are added (R-492, R-493, B1). `FormRole` gains `FORM_ROLE_PATTERN`, `FORM_ROLE_SEQUENCE`, `FORM_ROLE_EPISODE` and `FORM_ROLE_CODETTA` after `FORM_ROLE_LAYER` (`FORM_ROLE_COUNT` 13; `pattern`, `sequence`, `episode`, `codetta` in the role table, theme-like: bars 4 or 8 or a construction, built as `contrast` or `return` are). `FormSpec` gains `modulates`, read from the form document (`/modulates`; refused in a form whose sections the request gives and in a form with no `regions`) and in `schema/form.v1.schema.json`. `draw_keys` makes its home draw whenever the request modulates or the form does and a section of the size can leave home, and a form with `modulates` does not stay home on it, so the region draws after it are the same; `form_build_plan` plans the keys and the cadences with `request->modulate || form->modulates`. No built-in form uses either until B4, so every output is byte-identical. New: `test_form_spec modulates` (`modulating-form.json`: 9 of 10 compose in C major 3/4 and 10 of 10 in A minor, P in the key of V or v and S home in each) and the role and `modulates` tests in `test_form_spec`. See [forms.md](forms.md).

A liquidation section is grown from the pattern (R-494, B2). `FORM_ROLE_LIQUIDATION` follows `FORM_ROLE_CODETTA` (`FORM_ROLE_COUNT` 14; `liquidation` in the role table, theme-like) and `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_LIQUIDATION` (`{"kind": "liquidation"}`, no members) is a new constraint kind in plan JSON and `schema/plan.v2.schema.json`. The form reader takes a section of role `liquidation` of 4 bars and the half cadence only, and refuses `beginning`, `ending`, `construction`, `motive`, `returns`, `variation`, `pedal`, `leads_in`, `connective`, `layers` and `beats` on it; `form_build_plan` gives it the liquidation constraint in place of the archetype and the theme pairing, `compose_run` counts it as a theme section that is not heard as a theme shape, and the style leaves its form alone. New operator `grow_liquidate.c` (`GROW_LIQUIDATE_MAGIC`, dispatched from `grow_applies`, `grow_generate`, `grow_apply` and `grow_checker`): five ideas of 1, 1, 1, 1/2 and 1/2 bars over one prolongation of V with the pedal, the motive exact, one step down the key's scale, liquidated, then a scale line and a broken chord of V of its own that end the half cadence. No built-in form uses it until B4, so every output is byte-identical. New: `test_grow_liquidate` (`liquidation-form.json`: 10 of 10 seeds compose in C major 3/4, A minor 3/4, C major 2/4 and C major 6/8), the liquidation refusals in `test_form_spec` and the constraint round trip in `test_plan_json`. See [grow-liquidation.md](grow-liquidation.md) and [forms.md](forms.md).

## Unreleased (phase P48)

API 1.20 (R-485, R-486, B6). A row of a form with sizes gains `part`, `repeat_of`, `upbeat` and `connective` after `changes`; the piece document gains `performed` (`{"bars"}`, or `null`; absent for the game loop); the forms entry gains `meters`, `tempo`, `repeats`, `parts` and `performed`, and each section `leads_in` and `connective`. `form_return_edge_numerals` reads a section's first and last chord numerals. `piece.response.schema.json`, `piece_variations.response.schema.json` and `forms.schema.json` carry the members. Composed output unchanged: no plan, MIDI, MusicXML, snapshot or hash moved. Tests: the minuet and the minuet and trio rows in `test_api_piece`, the minuet, minuet and trio and ternary entries in `test_api_forms`. See [api/theme-and-piece.md](api/theme-and-piece.md), [api/vocabulary-and-validation.md](api/vocabulary-and-validation.md) and [minuet.md](minuet.md).

A form may list its meters and give a tempo (R-487, B1). `FormSpec` gains `meters` (1 to 4 of 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8; `meter_count`, 0 for any) and `tempo` (30 to 300 quarter notes a minute, 0 for none), read from the form document (`/meters/<i>`, `/tempo`; refused in a form whose sections the request gives) and in `schema/form.v1.schema.json`; `form_spec_meter_allowed` answers for a meter. `form_build_plan` refuses a meter the form does not list (`PIECE_ERR_BAD_REQUEST`), and the piece request refuses it at `/request/meter` (`the Minuet form is in 3/4`). A request with no tempo and no style tempo takes the form's, `tempo_from` `form` (the piece and variations response schemas allow `form`), and the MIDI and MusicXML carry it. No built-in form uses either member, so every output is byte-identical. New: `test_form_spec` (the reader, its refusals) and `test_form_spec_meters` (`meter-form.json` builds in 3/4, is refused in 4/4). See [forms.md](forms.md) and [piece-request.md](piece-request.md).

A section of a sized form may lead into the next (R-486, B2). `FormSectionSpec` gains `leads_in` and `connective` (`FORM_CONNECTIVE_NONE`, `SOMETIMES`, `ALWAYS`), read from the form document (`/sizes/<i>/sections/<j>/leads_in`, `connective`; refused on the last section, on a returning section, on a section another returns, on two in a row, and a connective without `leads_in`) and in `schema/form.v1.schema.json`. `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_CONNECTIVE` (`{"kind": "connective", "written": false}`) is a new constraint kind in plan JSON and `schema/plan.v2.schema.json`. `form_build_plan` holds a section that leads in and gives it a connective constraint (`always`, or `sometimes` drawn once per such section from the seed plus 7, so no existing plan moves); the seam stage now also fills held sections when the plan has no seam section, and `piece_connective_write` writes the stepwise connective into the next section after the melody stage. No built-in form uses either member, so every output is byte-identical. New: `test_form_spec leads` (`lead-form.json`: 10 of 10 compose in C major and A minor 3/4, the connective written in 7 and 6), the lead-in refusals in `test_form_spec`, and the connective round trip in `test_plan_json`. See [form-performance.md](form-performance.md).

A form may repeat groups of its sections and is performed in order (R-485, B3). `FormSpec` gains `repeats` (`FormRepeat {first, count}`, `repeat_count`), read from the form document (`/repeats/<i>/<j>`; refused for an unknown name, a group that is not consecutive and in order in every size, overlapping or unordered groups, and in a form whose sections the request gives) and in `schema/form.v1.schema.json`; `form_spec_performed` is true when a form has repeats. New public header `piece_perform.h`: `piece_perform` builds the performed plan of a composed piece (a form node, the meter, one section node for each performed section holding its source's letter, cadences and key, its key lane and its lanes Given by `plan_give_section`, each repeated group written out twice in place), with `PiecePerformPart`, `PiecePerformance` and `piece_performance_destroy`. `PieceVersion` gains `performance` and `performed`; for a form with repeats the piece entry answers with the performed plan (the `plans` member, the plan file, the MIDI file and the MusicXML), its rows are one for each performed section (the start and length of the performed section, every other member the source's), and the hearing members still come from the composed plan. The piece entry with `from` and the piece variations entry refuse such a form with `COMPOSER_ERR_REQUEST` at `/request/form`. No built-in form uses `repeats` until B5, so every output is byte-identical. New: `test_piece_perform` (`repeat-form.json`: 5 of 5 seeds compose in C major 4/4 and perform as A A B A' B A'; repeats do not change composing) and the repeats refusals in `test_form_spec`. See [form-performance.md](form-performance.md).

A compound form is made of parts, each composed on its own and performed in order (R-485, B4). `FormSpec` gains `parts` (`FormPart {name, form, key, new_motive, again, repeats}`, `part_count`, `FORM_SPEC_MAX_PARTS_OF` 4), read from the form document (`/parts/<i>/<member>`; refused for an unknown member, a form that is not a built-in form with sizes that is not itself compound or that has no size of each of the form's bars, an unknown region, an `again` naming a later, unknown or `again` part, `form`, `key` or `motive` with `again`, more than 4 parts, parts with `sections` in a size, `repeats` or a form whose sections the request gives) and in `schema/form.v1.schema.json` (a compound form's sizes have `bars` and no `sections`); `form_spec_performed` is also true with parts. `piece_region_key` (`piece_plan_keys.h`) is the old `pick_away` made public: a region's degree 1 is the home key's parallel key, any other degree the same closely related key as before, with the same draws, so no existing plan moves. `piece_perform.h` gains `PieceParts`, `piece_compose_parts` (the keys drawn first from the seed, then each part built and composed as a piece of its own form from the seed plus `PIECE_PART_SEED_STRIDE` times its index, with the piece's motive or an invented one), `piece_parts_destroy`, `piece_parts_source` and `piece_perform_compound` (the parts performed in order under the compound form's `plan_name`; an `again` part performs its source's plan without repeats unless it says so). `compose_piece` composes a compound form this way: the version has no plan and no compose outcome of its own (`PieceVersion.parts` points at the `PieceMade.parts` it reads), the plan, MIDI and MusicXML are the performed plan, the rows are one for each performed section from its part's composed plan and outcome, and the explanation is the first part's. No built-in form has parts until B5, so every output is byte-identical. New: `test_piece_perform parts` (`parts-form.json`: 10 of 10 seeds compose in C major 4/4, keys C major, A minor and C major, 48 bars of 6 sections, the third part's notes the first's moved, Second's m0 its own; `piece_region_key`) and the parts refusals in `test_form_spec`. See [form-performance.md](form-performance.md).

The minuet, the trio and the minuet and trio are built in (R-487, B5). `forms/minuet.json` (3/4 at 112, a sentence for A, B over a dominant pedal leading into A return with a connective on some seeds, `repeats` `[["A"], ["B", "A return"]]`), `forms/trio.json` (the minuet with a period for A and A return) and `forms/minuet-and-trio.json` (the minuet, the trio in its own region key with its own motive, the minuet da capo; 120 bars performed at 24) are in `COMPOSER_FORMS` between `layered` and `game-loop`: the forms list has 10 names. The bass operator makes 48 more attempts with the first chord's octave drawn when none of its first 48, which start nearest the middle of the range, passes the melodic rules (`build_line` with `free_first`, R-491): the B of the ternary, and so of the minuet, did not compose over a dominant pedal in G major and F# major (every line from G3 into the dominant's only D at D3 leaps or outlines a seventh); a span that has a line from the middle is unchanged, so nothing else moved. `piece.response.schema.json` allows a score denominator of 0 and `forms.schema.json` a size with no sections, as a compound form's piece and forms entry answer. New: [minuet.md](minuet.md) with the measured table (22 rows, 10 of 10 compose in every one), `test_form_templates_minuet*` and `test_form_templates_trio*` (baselines 35, 105, 30 and 36, 108, 45), `test_piece_keys_minuet` and `test_piece_keys_trio`, the built-in rows in `test_form_spec`, the minuet and the minuet and trio requests in `test_api_piece`, and `test_first_octave_fallback` in `test_harmony_bass`. See [minuet.md](minuet.md) and [forms.md](forms.md).

## Unreleased (phase L4)

The L4 settings are in the preferences document and the API is 1.19 (R-474, B6). The `melody` group gains `closure` (`final`, `first`), `rhythm_repeat_bars` (0 to 16), `leaps` and `register` (`closure_final`, `closure_first`, `rhythm_repeat_bars`, `leap_weight`, `register_weight` of `MelodyFillParams`), and the `development` group gains `contrast` (`distance` with `similar` and `distant`, `new_material`; `ComposerPreferences.contrast`, `GrowContrast`), each 0 to 20 unless stated, written in this order after `weights` and after `basic_as_written`; `distance` with both weights 0 reads as `similar` 1. Every member is off by default (R-422): the defaults write `closure` 0, `rhythm_repeat_bars` 0, `leaps` 0, `register` 0, `distance` 1 and 0, `new_material` 0, and a theme request with them composes the same theme as one with no preferences. The theme request passes `contrast` and the `motif` group to the grow run (`api_theme.c`), so the motif group now shapes a theme's drawn rhythms and new material and nothing else; `random_motive` accepts and echoes the new members. The vocabulary gains `limits.preferences.rhythm_repeat_bars_max` (16), and the schemas of the random motive and theme requests and responses and of the vocabulary gain the members and the theme response's slot `source` the values `distant` and `new`. Tests: `test_preferences` (the default document, each refusal with its path, every member read back equal), `test_api_vocabulary`, `test_api_theme` (new material 20 puts a `new` row in a period; every L4 member at its default equals no preferences), `test_listen_cli_smoke` (a settings file with every L4 member), and the version asserts at 1.19. See [preferences.md](preferences.md), [theme-request.md](theme-request.md), [cli-listen.md](cli-listen.md) and [api.md](api.md).

A theme's line can be weighted against leaps and for a steady register (R-481, B5). `MelodyFillParams.leap_weight` and `register_weight` (default 0, set from C only) score a candidate by the share of its intervals under 5 semitones (twice on a strong beat), and by its median pitch staying within 7 and 4 semitones of the idea before (`MelodyContext.before_register`, `check_line` in `melody_check.c`). They rank among the `keep` candidates and draw no random number; 0 changes nothing. Tests: `test_melody_preferences` (leap weight 20: 148 leaps against 149; register weight 20: 36 against 46).

A theme's line can be kept from repeating one bar's rhythm, and a diminution is kept readable (R-479, R-480, R-483, B4). `MelodyFillParams.rhythm_repeat_bars` (default 0, set from C only) caps the bars in a row of 2 or more notes with one rhythm: `MelodyContext` reads the run from the bars before the target (`melody_context_read_repeats`, `repeat_run`, `repeat_aligned`), and `limit_rhythm_repeats` varies a bar that passes the cap by merging its last two generated notes, or, when a motive note is among them, trading the lengths of its last two neighbouring notes of different lengths, never the target's last note, looking back to an earlier bar of the run when the bar cannot change. No random number; 0 changes nothing. A fragment that is a whole segment is no longer diminished when the diminution would write a note under a sixteenth (`diminution_readable`, `grow_ending.c`); `continuation.diminish` is off by default, so no default output moves. Tests: `test_melody_preferences` (cap 3 on the Test Motif sentence, seeds 1 to 8: longest run 3 against 6) and `test_grow_continuation` (`continuation/dotted.json`).

A phrase can end on purpose (R-478, B3). `MelodyFillParams.closure_final` and `closure_first` (default 0) weigh two soft rules for the target that ends the theme's last cadence and for one that ends an earlier cadence: the last note is at least twice the phrase's mean note value, and no note of the last bar is shorter than the note before it. `MelodyContext.last_cadence` marks the target that ends where its section ends. With a weight above 0, half the layouts of such a target (one random number, none at weight 0) settle into one long last note from the latest cell line that leaves twice the mean, and the bar before it merges a shorter note into the note before it; a note of the motive is never merged. Set from C only, with no change to an API output, and both weights 0 are byte-identical. Measured on the L4 baseline: periods' final note over the mean up to 2.05 (1.89), sentences' 2.71 to 3.72 (1.52 to 3.07). New: the closure tests in `test_melody_preferences`. See [melody-fill.md](melody-fill.md#closure-phase-l4-r-478).

A contrasting idea can be new material (R-477, B2). `GrowContrast.new_material` weighs it against the similar and distant weights, for the first contrasting idea of a theme with a contrasting slot (a period's antecedent, a compound basic idea) when no motive is given for the slot or the idea; `grow_draw_distance` becomes `grow_draw_contrast_kind` (`GrowContrastKind`: similar, distant, new), which draws no random number when only similar has weight. `grow_make_new_material` draws up to 8 motives from `motive_random` with the request's motif settings (the idea's one or two bars, refused when it keeps the basic idea's contour or does not fit MIDI 58 to 84) and records the first, preferring one that lands on the cadence's tone, as the contrasting slot's source: the material node `new material`. The first contrasting idea is that motive as it is and the consequent's contrasting idea is made from it, similar or distant by the weights. A failed make is the similar contrast. The material is sized for it (`GrowRequest.contrast.new_material`) and the theme's developments read `new material`, the slot rows made from it `new`. Set from C only, with no change to an API output, and every plan with `new_material` 0 is byte-identical. New: `new_material_recorded` and `new_material_composes` in `test_grow_contrast`. See [grow-contrast.md](grow-contrast.md).

A contrasting idea can be distant from the basic idea (R-475, R-476, B1). `GrowContrast` (`grow.h`) has `distance` (weights of `GROW_CONTRAST_SIMILAR` and `GROW_CONTRAST_DISTANT`; all 0 reads as similar 1) and `new_material` (B2's), carried in `GrowContext.contrast`, `GrowRequest.contrast` (the plan's material gets the bars a distant motive takes) and `GrowSetup.contrast`, and set from C only: the preferences document, vocabulary, schemas and API version follow in B6. `GrowContext.motif` and `GrowSetup.motif` carry the motif vocabulary (`MotiveRandomPreferences`, NULL for the defaults). With a distant weight, a period's contrasting idea, a consequent's contrasting idea and a sentence's fragment 1 may be a motive of its own, `distant for m<k>`: the contour change of today's contrast on a rhythm drawn by `motive_random` with the idea's bars, refused when it equals the basic idea's over their common length. A failed make keeps the similar contrast, and with every weight 0 nothing changes and no random number is drawn. The theme's developments name it `a distant variant` and its slot row has source `distant`. See [grow-contrast.md](grow-contrast.md).

## Unreleased (phase P47)

A theme may be uneven (R-468, R-471, B2). `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_CONSTRUCTION` (`"construction"` in the plan JSON and its schema) on a theme section gives the bars of each idea (`PlanConstruction`, `idea_bars` by `GrowIdea`, up to 3 extensions, a reason), and `GrowRequest.construction` adds it to the theme section. With one a theme is 3 to 16 bars, 4 or 8 without (`/bars` is refused otherwise), and `grow_layout_of` lays its ideas end to end in each half from the bars it gives (a construction with an extension is not laid out until B3). `grow_construction_buildable` and the refusals at `/construction/ideas` (the ideas are not the theme's bars, an idea the pairing does not have, an idea with no bar, a continuation fragment with as many bars as the basic idea) are beside `grow_theme_buildable`; each half's bars set its room for the cadence, the cadence bar of an antecedent, and the rooms of idea and slot motives. `grow_motive_fills_part` and `grow_part_basic_idea` take the part's and the basic idea's bars. A continuation of whole-bar fragments speeds its harmony when asked (`faster_harmony` reads the fragment's length, not 8 bars), and the label of an uneven theme names the halves (`sentence 4+5 bars`). With a construction a basic idea of 3 bars or any length outside 1, 2 and 4 only lowers the score (`judge_basic_idea`), and a contrasting idea shorter than its motive is made from the leading notes of that motive that fit (`grow_shorten_source`, the material node `shortened for m<place>`). Without a construction every plan, hash fixture, snapshot and recorded rate is byte-identical. New: `test_grow_uneven` (a 3+3, 5+5 and 4+6 period and a 4+5, 5+4 and 6+5 sentence compose 10 of 10 seeds in C major 4/4 and A minor 3/4; the refusals; the plan round trip). See [grow-construction.md](grow-construction.md).

A construction has the four extensions of ch. XIV (R-468, B3). Each `PlanExtension` is built: `repeat` (internal repetition) puts a new idea after the idea it repeats, its motive the repeated idea's in sequence one step down or, `varied`, with its last interval changed by 2 semitones; `stretch` lengthens the idea that ends the theme or an antecedent's contrasting idea; `addition` is a tonic phrase of 1 or 2 bars after the cadence whose melody is the trailing segment of the last idea; `evaded` makes the last idea arrive on a deceptive cadence (V to vi) and restates it exactly with the theme's cadence. `GrowLayout` gains `extensions`, `main_ending_bars`, `idea_beginning_bars` and `idea_ending_bars`; `grow_extensions_draw` makes the motives of repeats and additions (`GrowThemeSpec.extended`, the material nodes `extension <k>`), `build_parts` adds the repeat and evaded ideas in time order and `grow_add_chord_targets_over` writes the chords up to the deceptive cadence. `melody_cadence_mask` gives a deceptive cadence the tonic or the third. The refusals at `/construction/extensions/<k>` (a kind its idea cannot take, an HC to evade or add to, a second evaded cadence or addition, an addition over 2 bars) and the sum of bars count the extensions; the soft rules are asked on the ideas without them and the label names them. `faster_harmony` is left off for a construction's cadential idea or evaded idea a cadential progression of a chord a cell cannot fill. Without an extension every plan, hash fixture, snapshot and recorded rate is byte-identical. New: `test_grow_extensions` (an 8-bar sentence with a repeat of fragment 2, a stretched cadential idea, an addition or an evaded cadence, and a 4+6 period by a varied repeat, each 10 of 10 seeds in C major 4/4 and A minor 3/4; the layouts; the refusals). See [grow-construction.md](grow-construction.md).

A theme's construction is chosen from its motif or its length (R-469, B4). `grow_construction_choose` (`grow.h`) gives a theme of 3 to 16 bars with no construction one without a draw: the basic idea is the motif's bars from 3 (reason `motive`), else 2 from 6 bars up, else 1 (reason `length`); the halves follow, and the ending's excess becomes a stretch, a varied or sequential repeat, an evaded cadence or an addition. 6 bars make a 3+3 period, 9 a 4+5 sentence, 10 a 4+6 period or a 4+6 sentence with an evaded cadence, a 3-bar motif a 5+5 or 6+6 period; 4 and 8 bars with a motif of 2 bars or fewer get none and are built as ever. `grow_construction_describe` writes why as one sentence, `GrowOutcome.construction_text` and `ComposeHeard.construction_text` carry it, and `grow_build_plan` builds the chosen construction into the plan, refusing at `/bars` (`no construction makes a 2-bar period from this motif`) when there is none: a request for another length than 4 or 8 bars without a construction, refused before, is built now. Every plan with 4 or 8 bars, hash fixture, snapshot and recorded rate is byte-identical. New: `test_grow_construction`. See [grow-construction.md](grow-construction.md).

The theme and grow requests take a construction and the API is 1.18 (R-468, R-469, B5). `bars` is 3 to 16 (4 and 8 as before; any other length gets a construction chosen from the motif or the length), and a request member `construction`, `null` or `{"ideas": {"<idea name>": bars}, "extensions": [{"kind", "idea", "bars", "varied"}]}`, is a given construction (`GrowSetup.has_construction`, `api_grow_read_construction`); a refusal carries the grow refusal's path under `/bars` or `/construction` (`/construction/ideas/<idea>`, `/construction/extensions/<k>/<member>`). Each theme of the theme and grow responses gains `construction` (`null` for a theme without one, else `halves`, `ideas` with `first_bar`, `extensions` with `first_bar`, `reason` `given`, `motive` or `length`, and `text`, the sentence of `grow_construction_describe`), the root echoes a given construction, and the `theme` text output prints `construction: <text>`. The vocabulary lists `theme_bars` 3 to 16, `theme_bars_regular` (`[4, 8]`) and `theme_extensions` (`repeat`, `stretch` with their ideas, `addition`, `evaded`). `validate` reads and checks the construction as Compose does (it already built the plan without seeding). The schemas of the theme and grow requests and responses and of the vocabulary gain the members, and `tests/snapshots/theme-*.stdout.json` and `grow-*.stdout.json` the one line `"construction": null` in each theme; no other snapshot, hash fixture or recorded rate moved. New: `tests/fixtures/theme/uneven-period.json`, `uneven-sentence.json` and `evaded-sentence.json`, tests of the three through the API (`test_api_theme`), of a given construction and its refusals (`test_api_theme`, `test_api_validate`) and of the vocabulary's new members. See [theme-request.md](theme-request.md), [api/theme-and-piece.md](api/theme-and-piece.md), [grow-construction.md](grow-construction.md) and [api.md](api.md).

A form section and a game loop section may be uneven (R-468, R-470, B6). A section of a form document takes `construction`, `"auto"` or the theme request's object (`FormSectionSpec.has_construction`, `construction_auto`, `construction`; `schema/form.v1.schema.json`): the section is 3 to 16 whole bars, a theme-like section of other bars than 4 or 8 needs one (refused at `bars`), the ideas and extensions add up to the section's bars, and a returning section keeps its source's (`a return keeps the construction of <source>`). `form_build_plan` adds `PLAN_CONSTRAINT_CONSTRUCTION` to the section (for `"auto"` `grow_construction_choose` with the piece's motive, `PIECE_ERR_BAD_REQUEST` when none fits; an open motive counts as one bar), takes the motive's basic idea bars from the constructions and adds the repeats' and addition's material bars; `piece_basic_idea` reads them too.
