# Changelog

The Composer versions and what each changed. Back to the [docs index](README.md). Older entries are in the [changelog archive](changelog-archive.md).

## Unreleased (phase P70)

API 4.10 (R-761, P70 B4): a theme's, blueprint's or piece section's given chords may start on any beat or half beat, up to four a bar, in every meter (the error "a bar of 4/4 takes one chord" and "a chord starts only every 2 beats" are gone; five chords are refused with `chords bar <n>: write 1 to 4 chords, not 5`). A span is read on the beat or division cells only when a Given chord starts or ends off the accent cells, so every request accepted before composes byte for byte as it did ([harmony-lab.md](harmony-lab.md)). The vocabulary's meters gain `chords_max` (4). The capacities of a theme's and a blueprint's given chords are 32 and 64. Chord text may name a mode after a colon (R-762, P70 B5): `IV:dorian`, `II:lydian`, `V7:mixolydian@2` is the chord on that degree of the mode, read like a borrowed chord of the nearest mode that holds it; it needs the knowledge snapshot, an upper-case numeral with no `b`, `#` or color, and a mode other than the key's own. `MotiveSketchChord` gains `mode`.

## Unreleased (phase P69c)

API 4.9 (R-749 to R-752, P69c B2 and B3): the preferences' `blueprint` group ([preferences-blueprint.md](preferences-blueprint.md); the vocabulary's preference limits gain `blueprint_range_min`, `blueprint_range_max` and `blueprint_candidates_max`), and the theme `development` `blueprint`: the Composer writes the theme's blueprint from the motif and realizes it ([blueprint-generator.md](blueprint-generator.md)). Each theme of its response has `blueprint`, the blueprint it wrote, in the form a request's blueprint reads. The random motive's `strategy` `blueprint` (R-753) draws a two-bar motif from the K8 expectations of the sentence's basic idea and the blueprint preferences ([cli-random-motive.md](../../cli/docs/cli-random-motive.md#the-blueprint-strategy-r-753)); the vocabulary's `random_motive_strategies` has the third row. Presets (R-754, B5): a theme or random motive request takes `preset`, the name of a built-in settings document (`ff7` or `hollow-knight`, [presets.md](presets.md)) whose settings its `preferences` apply over; the vocabulary has `presets`, and the theme response's `brief` echoes `preset`. The critic has two more evaluators, `register_shift` and `half_notes` (R-755; `critic.weights` and `critic.reverse` take their codes), and with `blueprint.candidates` above 1 a generated blueprint's candidates are ranked by it. `blueprint` is the default development of a theme request that fits it (R-756; the vocabulary's `defaults.theme.development`; one that cannot be written that way stays balanced). The vocabulary's `theme_developments` has the fourth row, and `ursatz listen --development blueprint` asks for it. A request that is not an 8-bar sentence or period from a motif of up to 2 bars in a major or natural minor key in 4/4, 3/4 or 6/8 is refused at `/development`. Measured on the four motif fixtures, as sentences and periods, seeds 1 to 10: every case composes on 10 of the 10 seeds ([blueprint-generator.md](blueprint-generator.md#measured-p69c-gate-2)).

## Unreleased (phase P69b)

API 4.8 (R-743, R-744, P69b B2 and B6): a theme request's `blueprint` ([blueprints.md](blueprints.md)) gives the theme bar by bar (chords, melody, rhythm or job, anchors, events, bass, inner voices, climax, accompaniment), and then `motives` and `slots` are optional. The chord text of a theme's `chords`, a motive's `harmony` and a section's chords reads lower-case numerals (`iv`, `v` in a minor key), `o`, `+`, `M`/`maj7`, applied chords (`V7/V`) and the suffixes `sus2` and `sus4`; every text that read before reads the same. `MotiveSketchChord` gains `quality_given`, `quality`, `has_target`, `target_degree`, `target_alteration` and `target_minor`.

Accompaniment patterns (R-745, P69b B1 and B3): the knowledge snapshot has `pattern` entries (`rhythm`, `accompaniment`; K11). An `accompaniment.pattern` may name an accompaniment entry by its id (today the five `michelle.pattern.*`), which the plan writes as `"pattern": "entry"` with `pattern_id`; the vocabulary's `accompaniment_patterns` lists them. `block`, `arpeggiated`, `alberti` and `oom-pah` write what they wrote.

Melody constraints (R-746, P69b B4 and B5): the plan constraints `anchor`, `event` and `climax` on a melody span, held by the melody stage and refused by its checker when broken; rhythm-only idea motives (`composer_rhythm_motive_attach`) and the blueprint motive (`composer_blueprint_motive`). Without them every output is as it was.

Blueprint text (R-747, P69b B7): a blueprint written as Ursatz Text (`ursatz-text 1 blueprint`), and `ursatz listen --blueprint`.

Reference blueprints (R-740, P69b B8): `tests/fixtures/references` holds the reference themes and their blueprints; `test_reference_blueprints` renders each full blueprint note for note as its reference, `test_reference_skeletons` (slow) and its smoke compose each skeleton on seeds 1 to 10 with every fixed item held ([blueprints.md](blueprints.md#the-reference-blueprints)). A colored tonic in root position (`iadd9`) may end a PAC; `i6` and `i64` still may not.

## Unreleased (phase P69)

API 4.7 (R-725, P69 B4): a key's material may be one of the church modes: `"dorian"`, `"lydian"`, `"mixolydian"`, `"phrygian"` or `"locrian"` (the `material` of the theme, piece, plan, grow, counterpoint and random motive requests). The old `mode` member stays major or minor. A region in a piece may be in the home's mode only, or change between major and minor ([modes.md](modes.md)). A word the Composer does not write is refused with "the Composer writes major, minor and modal keys; '<word>' is not one of them". The seventh is raised to a leading tone in the natural minor only; a motive in D natural minor is no longer a motive in D dorian.


Color notes (R-732, P69 B5): `melody.color_note` (0 to 20, default 2) and `motif.color_note` (0 to 8, default 4) move a modal theme's melody and random motif onto the mode's characteristic steps; major and minor keys have none.

Modal mixture and brightness (R-729, R-730, P69 B6 and B7): `harmony.mixture` (`share`, `from`; off by default) borrows predominant chords from other modes on the tonic, the theme response's chord rows gain `borrowed_from`, and the plan's harmony lane keeps it. The piece response's section rows gain `brightness` (`{"numerator", "denominator"}`, null for a section with no notes); a region may name any mode the Composer writes, and a theme given to a section must be in the section's material.

Color chords (R-727, P69 B8): a numeral takes a suffix (`Iadd9`, `IV69`, `V9`, `Imaj9`, `ii9`, `Vq3`) that names a chord type of the knowledge snapshot; the chord sounds the type's members while every check reads the basic chord. `harmony.color` (`share`, `types`) and `motif.color_chords` (a ratio; off by default) draw them, `motif.plan` and the motive harmony text (`IVadd9@2`) and the given `chords` of a theme or piece request take the suffix. Preference keys are 149 with the workshop's two.

Voicings (R-731, P69 B9 and B10): a theme or piece request's `accompaniment` takes `"voicing": "<id>"` with the pattern `block`; the vocabulary lists `voicings` (`value`, `label`, `fits`), and the materials lists (`materials`, `piece_materials`, and the new `region_materials`) hold major, natural minor and the five church modes from the darkest. The workshop reads the `knowledge` folder of the data folder as user knowledge when it opens.

Modal sweep (P69 B11): `test_modal_sweep_<mode>` (slow) and `test_modal_sweep_smoke` hold twenty sentences and twenty periods in each church mode to the gate-2 targets, and `test_piece_r611` the R-611 ternary from C locrian to two steps brighter.

## Unreleased (phase P68)

Ranking by the critic (R-722, P68 B5): with `critic.rank` on, a theme request makes `critic.candidates` themes for each one it keeps, judges them together and keeps the best; the response gains `critic` in each theme and `rejected` in the document (`theme.response.schema.json`, `grow.response.schema.json`). `GrowSetup.critic`, `GrowTheme.judged` and `.judgment`, `GrowRun.rejected`, `.ranked`. `ursatz listen` varies the generated motifs of a theme batch when `candidates.variety` is above 0. With rank off every output is as it was.

API 4.6 (R-721, P68 B4): `composer_api_critique(context, request_json, job, out)` judges one to 16 candidates (a plan or a motive document each) with the critic and answers a score, each evaluator's penalty, weight and reversal, and the observations in plain words ([critic.md](critic.md#the-critique-entry); schemas `critique.request.schema.json` and `critique.response.schema.json`). The critic (B2, B3) has the `critic` group of the preferences (12 keys, 8 rules) and nothing composes with it yet. With the group's `rank` off every output is as it was.

## Unreleased (phase P67)

Theme quality sweep (R-543, P67 B8): `test_theme_functions_sweep` grows the four harmony fixtures as sentences and periods with every function setting and `melody.cadence_long` on, seeds 1 to 10 (10 of 10 compose in each case), and checks the predominant before the final dominant, the held last note, the contrasts and two chords in a 4/4 sentence's fragment bars. The measure is in [testing/theme-quality.md](testing/theme-quality.md). A contrast target's motive is drawn no longer than the material is sized for, and the response's `contrasts` read the ideas' motives, not the melody. No output changes with the settings off.

Surface by function (R-712, P67 B7): `development.functions.surface` draws a sentence's fragments by their formal function: fragment 1 the fitting segment of m0 with the most notes per beat, fragment 2 the next segment, exact (the same one in sequence when only one fits), no random number. `melody.cadence_long` (`MelodyFillParams.cadence_long`, hard, off by default; 128 preference keys, 21 hard) holds the last note of a target that ends a cadence from its last downbeat to its end, a restated motive's notes from there on not kept; a melody that does otherwise is refused by a check of its own. API 4.5. Off, every plan is as it was.

Harmony by function (R-711, P67 B6): with `development.functions.harmony` an antecedent, a continuation and a consequent of a theme with no construction take the harmony of their ideas' formal functions (`grow_function_harmony.c`): the cadential idea a `cadential` target that starts earlier when it has too few cells before its last bar, the fragments a chord a cell with `free_motive`, the basic ideas a prolongation from the tonic. The harmony shape of a plan gains `free_motive` (written only when true; plan format 6). `GrowThemeRequest.given_second_half`, `GrowThemeSpec.function_type` and `function_harmony`. Off, every plan is as it was. Over s77-chords, u51-chords and the 3/4 motif, 8 of 8 seeds compose as sentences and as periods.

API 4.5 (R-710, P67 B5): the development preferences take `functions` (`harmony`, `surface`, `contrast`, booleans, off by default; 127 keys, three more, all soft) and, with `functions.contrast`, a contrasting idea of an antecedent, a compound basic idea or a consequent is drawn as a distant contrast that meets its formal function's contrast target (rhythm and contour by 1/2) or the structure is dropped. The plan constraint `contrast` (`against`, `aspects`; plan format 6) holds the target and the theme response has `contrasts` (`idea`, `against`, `rhythm`, `contour`, `register`, `density`, measured on the ideas' motives). `harmony` and `surface` are read and written and act from B6 and B7. `GrowFunctions`, `GrowContext.functions` and `.knowledge`. The development preferences moved to `preferences_development.c`. Off, every output is as it was.

Sequence presentation (R-714, P67 B3; A20261115-81): a sequence repetition over a motif with chords holds the motif's chords a step up (I to ii, V to vi), as R-348 says; `build_presentation` read `spec->source`, which never carries chords, so the guard was always true and the chords were written whole (the tonic and the second degree). The guard reads the compose run's basic motive and no section motive. Default themes whose motif has chords change; the API, the plan format and the schemas are as they were.

## Unreleased (phase P66)

API 4.4 (R-693, P66b B9): `composer_api_validate` of a motive with `"stored": true` refuses a key but C major or C minor at `/params/body` (a stored motif is written with C as the tonic); the motive request of `composer_api_motive_from_midi` takes `write_in_c`, and its analysis then has `key_text` and `notes_text_in_c`; the vocabulary gives `beat_unit` in each meter, `defaults` and `theme_form_members`; the schema ids name this repository.

API 4.3 (R-672, P66 B2): the melody group of the preferences takes `waive` (hard rules the melody stage does not hold: `forbidden-leap`, `forbidden-outline`, `no-crossing`, `parallel-octaves`, `parallel-fifths`, `dissonance-against-bass`, `strong-beat-chord-tone`) and `reverse` (soft rule groups scored by what they break), both empty by default; a response that echoes the preferences writes both. The vocabulary lists `preference_keys` (every setting by its dotted key, with its type, limits, class and default) and `rules`. `ComposerPreferenceKey` and `ComposerRule` with `composer_preferences_keys` and `composer_preferences_rules` (`<ursatz_composer/preferences.h>`); `MelodyFillParams.waive` and `reverse`. The preferences schema is one file, `schema/api/preferences.schema.json`, that the theme and random motive requests and responses and the vocabulary refer to (B1). Without preferences every output is unchanged.

API 4.3 (R-673, P66 B3): the preferences take a fourth group, `candidates` (`variety` 0 to 20, default 0; `pool` 2 to 8, default 4), and the random motive request takes `avoid`, an array of up to 64 motive documents (refused at `/avoid/<i>`). With variety above 0 each motive is chosen among `pool` candidates (at least the style's 8) for being unlike the motives of `avoid` and the ones already made; with variety 0 the draw is as it was. `limits.preferences` gains `pool_min` and `pool_max`; `MotiveRandomOptions` gains `variety`, `pool`, `avoid` and `avoid_count`. `ursatz listen --what motifs` hands a varied batch's earlier motifs to each sample.

## Unreleased (phase P64)

The Composer's command line is `ursatz` (R-657, R-658): `ursatz-composer` is gone, `export` is `ursatz render`, `--json` is `--format json`, and the command pages moved to the [cli docs](../../cli/docs/README.md). The API and its schemas are as they were.

## Unreleased (phase P63)

API 4.2 (R-651, P63 B16): each section of a form in `forms` has `labels` (`role`, `return`, `keys`, `figure`) and each size a `label`; each row of a piece version has `start_text` (`"bar 3"`, `"bar 3 beat 2"`) and `length_text` (`"2 bars"`, `"1 bar 2 beats"`) in the request's meter, the loop region has `text` and `length_text`, and the seam has `rules` (`id`, `label`, `ok`). The vocabulary's limits have `construction_bars` (`{"min": 3, "max": 16}`) and `construction_pickup` (`false`). The members are additions: a plan and every composed file are as before.

API 4.1 (R-649, P63 B14): the vocabulary lists `theme_developments` (`value`, `label`, `help`) and `lanes` and the limits `theme_motives` (16), `theme_extensions` (3), `piece_variations` (8) and `harmony_cells` (8), so a client holds no copy of them.

API 4.0 (R-639, P63 B2): the Composer answers with the common status set. Every entry returns `UrsatzStatus` with an `UrsatzError` (`message` and `path`); `ComposerStatus`, `ComposerError` and `ComposerJob` are gone and a long call takes the common `UrsatzJob` (`<common/job.h>`: progress and cancelled callbacks). A request says `"api_version": 4` or none; the versions response names `producer_key` where it had `ursatz_commit`. The two `export_failure` copies are one `api_export_failure`: a motive export refusal is `REQUEST` at `/midi` or `/musicxml`, its message prefixed with "the motive cannot be exported". `composer_api.h` includes `<common/status.h>` and `<common/job.h>`. The Composer exports only its API (27 names, `cmake/UrsatzExports.cmake`, R-640) and calls `common/` for its files, JSON, strings and hash (R-638).
